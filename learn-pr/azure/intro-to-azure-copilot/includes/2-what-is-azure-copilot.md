Azure Copilot is an AI-powered, chat-based tool that helps you do more with Azure. By using Azure Copilot, you can gain new insights, discover more benefits of the cloud, and orchestrate across both cloud and edge. It combines large language models (LLMs), the Azure control plane, and insights about your own Azure environment to help you work more efficiently.

## The problem it solves

Azure offers hundreds of services and thousands of resource types. Finding the right service, learning how it works, and stitching together the tools to design, operate, and troubleshoot your workloads takes time and expertise. Azure Copilot helps you navigate that complexity. It unifies knowledge and data across hundreds of services to increase productivity, reduce costs, and provide deep insights.

Instead of remembering exact service names, command syntax, or where a setting lives, you express your goals in natural language. Azure Copilot interprets your intent and helps you get the answer or complete the task. This approach simplifies your Azure management experience and lowers the learning curve for working with the cloud.

## Tailored to your environment

Azure Copilot does more than answer general questions. It can provide information tailored to your own Azure resources and environment. For example, it can summarize the resources in a subscription, explain a recommendation for a specific resource, or help you understand a service health event that affects your workloads. Because it can reason over both Azure documentation and your environment, the guidance you receive is relevant to your situation.

## Where you can use it

You can access Azure Copilot in two places:

- The **Azure portal**, by selecting the **Copilot** icon in the page header.
- The **Azure mobile app**, so you can work with Azure Copilot on the go.

Azure Copilot is available in 19 languages and is included at no extra cost for the capabilities available today.

> [!NOTE]
> Azure Copilot isn't available in national clouds. By default, all users in a tenant can access Azure Copilot, but Global Administrators can choose to control access for their organization.
