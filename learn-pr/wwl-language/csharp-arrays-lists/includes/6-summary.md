In this module, you learned how to organize related data using C#'s two most fundamental collection types.

You used **arrays** to store a fixed-size sequence of items and access them by index, and `List<T>` collections to store a resizable sequence of items and add, remove, or update them as your program runs. You compared the two and learned when each is the better fit for a problem.
