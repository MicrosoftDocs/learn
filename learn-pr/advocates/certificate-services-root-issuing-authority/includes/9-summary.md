In this module, you learned to deploy and harden an offline root CA and enterprise issuing CAs, prepare `CAPolicy.inf`, and complete controlled signing procedures. You also learned to configure AIA/CDP and CRLs, publish CA artifacts to AD DS and HTTP, manage root trust and NTAuth, validate certificate chains, capture configuration baselines, back up the CA database and keys, and plan CA renewal.
