A guarded fabric works only when its authority boundaries survive normal operation, change, outage, and recovery. TPM attestation determines whether a Hyper-V host meets approved security conditions. A VM's shielded policy and KP separately determine its protections and authorized fabrics. Neither decision substitutes for the other.

Owner-approved images, protected provisioning data, and independent key custody extend that boundary into deployment. Servicing and key rollover preserve it only when old and new states are deliberately managed. Recovery must restore both usable cryptographic relationships and the intended trust decisions, not merely make a VM boot.
