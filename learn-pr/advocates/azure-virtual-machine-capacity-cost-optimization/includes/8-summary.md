You evaluated rightsizing as a workload, allocation, change-control, and financial decision. A defensible outcome can be an accepted canary, a rejected recommendation, or a justified deferral. Savings become credible only after comparable usage, commitment allocation, unused benefits, and residual costs are reconciled.
