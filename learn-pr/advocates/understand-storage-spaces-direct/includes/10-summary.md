Storage Spaces Direct turns local drives into resilient cluster storage. Production success depends on the complete stack:

- Validated symmetric hardware.
- Durable cache devices.
- Sufficient repair reserve.
- Redundant low-latency SMB paths.
- Appropriate mirror or parity placement.
- ReFS volume design.
- Repeatable validation.
- Health, performance, capacity, and reliability monitoring.
- Controlled maintenance.
- Evidence-driven troubleshooting.
