Before your team commits to a platform, you need evidence that the platform fits the application. When you build on data that lives in Microsoft Fabric, that evidence comes from two places: a clear view of what Fabric Apps provides and limits, and a working prototype that runs in a real workspace.

You're asked to prototype an internal dashboard for regional sales managers at Adventure Works Cycles. The data already lives in Fabric. Before choosing a design, you need to know what a Fabric app includes, which constraints apply, whether the tenant is ready, and what the app costs to run. A deployed prototype answers those questions faster than a design document.

In this module, you explore how a Fabric app runs on Microsoft Fabric and how its child services relate to the Rayfin CLI and SDK. You learn how to weigh capabilities, constraints, licensing, and capacity cost against a scenario. Hands-on exercises then take you through the full path: scaffold a project, run it against a Fabric-hosted backend, deploy it with the Rayfin CLI, and verify the result.

By the end of this module, you're able to decide whether Fabric Apps fits an application scenario by deploying a working prototype to Microsoft Fabric.
