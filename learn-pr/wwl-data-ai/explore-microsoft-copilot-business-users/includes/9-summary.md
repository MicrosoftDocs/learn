In this module, you learned how AI can support everyday work, why grounding helps improve results, how AI agents can assist with complex tasks, and why human review remains an important part of using AI with confidence.

You also learned how to use Microsoft Copilot to:

- Create content
- Analyze information
- Generate ideas
- Support decision-making
- Identify when AI agents can help
- Work with confidence and responsibility

As you continue your AI journey, look for opportunities to experiment with these capabilities in your own work. With practice, you can develop the skills and confidence to use AI as a valuable partner in helping you achieve more.
