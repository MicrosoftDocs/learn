In this module, you learned:

- Distinguish Managed Training from Programmed Training (preview), separately from fine-tuning methods.
- Recognize when adapting a model's weights addresses an agent quality problem, rather than changing prompts, tools, or retrieval.
- Select model-specific Managed Training methods including supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement fine-tuning, and direct preference optimization.
- Prepare training data, including synthetic examples, that meets format and quality requirements.
- Design an optimization strategy using baseline evaluation, measurable targets, dataset splits, and supported training hyperparameters.
- Apply method-selection decisions to Adventure Works scenarios in an interactive exercise without running a live training job.
