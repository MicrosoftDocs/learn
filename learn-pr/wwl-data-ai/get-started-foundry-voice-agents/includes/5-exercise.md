Now it's your opportunity to build a voice agent. In this exercise, you create a voice-based agent in the Foundry portal, configure its listening and speaking behavior, write speech-optimized instructions, and test it with realistic scenarios.

> [!NOTE]
> If you don't have an Azure subscription, and you want to explore Microsoft Foundry, you can [sign up for an account](https://azure.microsoft.com/pricing/purchase-options/azure-account?cid=msft_learn), which includes credits for the first 30 days.

Launch the exercise and follow the instructions.
