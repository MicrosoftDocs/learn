AI applications can reason over complex problems, speak naturally, understand recorded conversations, and create or edit visual content. Each of these workloads has different requirements, so choosing a model begins with understanding the modality and outcome you need.

Microsoft AI develops the **MAI** family of models for real-world work. The portfolio includes foundational models for reasoning and content generation, along with specialized models for tasks such as transcription. These models are designed to amplify human capability while providing practical options for quality, latency, and cost.

## Keep humanity at the center

Microsoft AI describes its vision as **humanist superintelligence**: building advanced AI systems that remain controllable, aligned, and firmly in service of humanity. Its values connect how AI teams work with the systems they create. They emphasize:

- Communicating with care and making space for different perspectives.
- Earning trust through consistency, honesty, and clarity about both strengths and limitations.
- Building models that are accurate, reliable, and useful for work that matters.
- Designing clear experiences that let people focus on their work rather than the underlying technology.
- Treating responsibility and capability as complementary, with privacy, security, and human rights considered throughout development.
- Testing rigorously, listening closely, measuring results, and improving continuously.

These values provide a useful foundation for evaluating a model. Capability matters, but so do control, transparency, reliability, safety, and the effect the resulting system has on people.

In this module, you'll explore Microsoft AI models for four categories of workload:

- **Chat and thinking** - Solve problems that require multi-step reasoning, mathematics, coding, or enterprise analysis.
- **Code generation** - Plan and implement software changes across agentic development workflows.
- **Speech and transcription** - Generate expressive speech from text or convert spoken audio into accurate text.
- **Image generation** - Create and edit photorealistic, design-ready visual content.

You'll also learn how variants within a model family can help you choose between maximum quality and faster, lower-cost operation.

Learn more about the vision and values on the [Microsoft AI home page](https://microsoft.ai?azure-portal=true).
