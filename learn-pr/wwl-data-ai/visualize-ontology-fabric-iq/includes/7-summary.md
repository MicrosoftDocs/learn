In this module, you explored the Lamna Healthcare ontology, discovering how bound data appears as entity instances and how those instances connect through the relationship graph.

You opened entity type details and used **Instances** to inspect records and property values from bound sources. The ontology canvas shows definitions, while instances show the associated data.

You selected eligible entity types and relationships for optional graph materialization, then used **Explore graph** to view the connected records. Graph results depend on supported sources and the graph's last refresh.

You used a queryset to filter and explore the graph. Components identify the available entity and relationship types, while path queries use direction, hop counts, and property filters to find connected records.

With visualization and exploration in place, you've completed the core workflow: define business vocabulary, bind it to data, and explore eligible connected data to answer questions without writing joins or navigating raw schemas.
