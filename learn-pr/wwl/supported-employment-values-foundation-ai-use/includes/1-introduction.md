Supported employment helps people obtain and succeed in paid jobs in inclusive workplaces. Job coaches help employees identify employment goals, learn routines, overcome workplace barriers, build skills, and use supports that fit their needs. Support may reduce over time, but the right level of ongoing support depends on the employee and the job. Supported Employment also supports employers as an equal partner, to understand how to build inclusive workplaces where people with disabilities can flourish. 

AI can help with parts of this work, especially drafting and organizing. It does not change the purpose of job coaching. The employee leads decisions about their work and support. The job coach brings observation, relationships, local knowledge, and professional judgment.
