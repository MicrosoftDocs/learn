Before using Copilot, a person can ask:

- What do I need help with?
- Could Copilot make this task easier?
- Do I have enough information for a useful prompt?
- Do I have time to check the answer?
- Would another support work better?

## Use AI when it adds value

AI may be useful when a person wants help getting started, organizing information, or creating a draft.

For example:

> I have three school assignments due this week. Help me make a plan.

Copilot can suggest a plan. The student reviews the due dates and decides whether to use it.
