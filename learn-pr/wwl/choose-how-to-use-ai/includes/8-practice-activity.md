Practice choosing the support that fits a real situation.

## Step 1: Read the situation

You have a job interview next week for a grocery store position. You want to feel prepared, but you are not sure what to do first.

## Step 2: Think about your options

What could help you prepare?

- Ask Copilot for common interview questions
- Practice with a job coach, family member, or trusted person
- Write down your answers to common questions
- Use more than one support

## Step 3: Choose your support

Which option would you use? There may be more than one good answer.

**Example:**

- Use Copilot to find interview practice questions
- Practice answering the questions with a job coach
- Write down a few important points to remember

## Step 4: Think about your decision

Answer these questions:

- Why did you choose this support?
- How could AI help?
- How could another person help?
- Would you use one support or more than one?
- What would you do if the AI response was not helpful?
