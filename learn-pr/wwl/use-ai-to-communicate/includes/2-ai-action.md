A person wants to tell their supervisor that they finished a task but is not sure how to start the message.

They open Copilot and type:

> Help me write a message to my supervisor. Tell them I finished stocking the shelves.

Copilot might respond:

> Hello, I finished stocking the shelves. Please let me know if there is anything else I can help with.

The person can send the message, change it, or ask Copilot for another version.
