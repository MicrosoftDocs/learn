AI can help people:

- Start a message
- Make a message shorter
- Use clearer words
- Ask for help
- Organize ideas before writing

AI creates a draft. People decide what to send.

## Key takeaways

- AI can help with writing. 
- AI can help create clear messages. 
- You can change what AI writes. 
- Read a message before sending it. 
- You decide what to send.
