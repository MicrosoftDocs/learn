Using AI effectively is about following a consistent process that helps you get reliable results while protecting employee privacy, maintaining quality, and supporting informed decision-making. This five-step workflow can be used across many common job coaching tasks.

1. Choose the right task. Select work that is repeated, draft-based, and appropriate for AI.
1. Prepare permitted inputs. Remove information the tool does not need and follow organizational policy.
1. Prompt clearly. Use role, task, audience, format, and constraints.
1. Review and refine the content using the standards from AI Basics for Job Coaches and the responsible AI checklist from Using AI Responsibly in Supported Employment.
1. Decide, involve, and save. Confirm the final version with the right people, then save the prompt if it worked.
