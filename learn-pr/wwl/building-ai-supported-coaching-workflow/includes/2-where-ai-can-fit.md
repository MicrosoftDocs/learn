Supported employment stages and terminology vary. The examples below are a guide, not a universal process. Follow your local model and organizational policy. Review the [Supported Employment Glossary](https://aka.ms/SupportedEmploymentGlossary) for definitions and terminology use.

| **Activity** | **AI may help** | **People lead** |
|---|---|---|
| **Engagement and planning** | Draft accessible explanations, agendas, or conversation questions. | Build trust, understand goals, and support informed choice. |
| **Vocational profiling or discovery** | Organize employee-approved content or create an accessible version. | Observe, listen, develop the full profile, and confirm meaning and sharing choices. |
| **Employer engagement and job development** | Draft outreach options, research questions, or meeting notes from approved information. | Select outreach, understand business needs, and build relationships. |
| **Job matching and job analysis** | Compare verified task requirements with employee-approved strengths and skill-building needs. | Determine fit, analyze the job, and decide whether a skills gap can be addressed. |
| **In-work support and career progression** | Draft task analyses, visual supports, prompting plans, and scenarios. | Coach, observe, build coworker supports, adjust support, and plan progression. |
| **Documentation and follow-up** | Improve the clarity of permitted, non-identifying drafts or turn notes into action items. | Follow agency documentation rules and verify the final record. |
