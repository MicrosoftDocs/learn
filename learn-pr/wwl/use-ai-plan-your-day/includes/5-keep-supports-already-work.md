Copilot does not need to replace a calendar, planner, or visual schedule. A person might:

- Ask Copilot to help organize a busy day
- Review and change the plan
- Copy the final times into a calendar
- Add reminders for important activities
- Ask a trusted person to check the plan

Other supports can include:

- A paper planner
- A phone calendar
- Reminder alarms
- A visual schedule
- Written notes
- Support from a family member, teacher, job coach, or another trusted person

AI can be used with any of these supports.
