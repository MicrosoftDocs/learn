Copilot can help organize the information a person provides. A useful plan includes:

- Activities that need to happen
- Known times
- Travel time
- Breaks
- Due dates
- A time to finish

Review the plan before using it. Add missing information and change anything that does not fit the day.

## Key takeaways

- AI can help make schedules and checklists. 
- Adding times and details can make a plan more useful. 
- AI can help decide what to do first. AI can break a large task into smaller steps. 
- A plan can be changed when the day changes. 
- AI can work with calendars, reminders, planners, and other supports. 
- The person decides which suggestions to use.
