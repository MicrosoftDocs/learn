Most days include activities that happen at certain times. 

A person may need to:

- Arrive at work or school on time
- Travel to an appointment, work or school
- Complete homework or chores
- Eat lunch
- Take a break
- Pick up something at a store
- Be home at a certain time

Copilot can help put this information into a schedule. The schedule is a starting point. The person checks that the plan works.
