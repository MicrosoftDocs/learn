These prompts are ready to copy into Copilot. They can be changed to fit the day.

| **Use** | **Prompt** |
|---|---|
| **Plan a workday** | I work from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and have an appointment at 2:00 PM. It takes 30 minutes to get there. Add time for lunch and help me plan my day. |
| **Prepare for work** | I start work at 9:00 AM. Make a checklist of what I should do before I leave for work. |
| **Plan schoolwork** | I have math homework due tomorrow and a science quiz on Friday. I have one hour tonight. Help me decide what to do first. |
| **Plan an appointment** | I have an appointment at 2:00 PM. The bus ride takes 35 minutes. Help me decide when to leave. |
| **Plan a morning** | I need to do laundry, clean my room, and buy groceries before noon. Help me plan my morning. |
| **Break down a large task** | Break cleaning my room into short steps that I can complete one at a time. |
| **Add a break** | Add a 15-minute break to this schedule: [paste the schedule]. |
| **Update a plan** | My appointment moved from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Update this plan: [paste the plan]. |
| **Make a checklist** | Turn this schedule into a checklist: [paste the schedule]. |
| **Prepare for tomorrow** | Make a checklist for tomorrow morning. I need to leave home at 8:00 AM for school. |
