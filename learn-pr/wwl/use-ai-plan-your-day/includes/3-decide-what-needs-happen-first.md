Sometimes several tasks need to be completed, but they do not all have the same importance. For example, a student may need to:

- Finish an assignment due tomorrow
- Study for a quiz next week
- Pack a backpack
- Choose clothes for the morning

The student could type:

> I have an assignment due tomorrow and a quiz next week. I also need to pack my backpack. What should I do first?

Copilot can suggest an order. The student can change the order based on what is most important.
