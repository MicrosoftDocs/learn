After Copilot creates a plan, one short follow-up prompt can ask for a change.

## Prepare for work

| **Prompt** | **I start work at 9:00 AM. Make a checklist of what I should do before I leave for work.** |
|---|---|
| **Copilot might respond** | Check your schedule. Get dressed. Pack lunch. Check transportation. Bring your work badge. |
| **Follow-up prompt** | Add the time I should leave. |
| **Remember** | Add anything important that Copilot does not know about. |

## Plan schoolwork

| **Prompt** | **I have math homework due tomorrow and a science quiz on Friday. I have one hour tonight. Help me make a plan.** |
|---|---|
| **Copilot might respond** | Work on math for 40 minutes. Study science for 20 minutes. |
| **Follow-up prompt** | Add a 10-minute break. |
| **Remember** | Check the due dates and change the plan if needed. |

## Plan an appointment

| **Prompt** | **I have an appointment at 2:00 PM. The bus ride takes 35 minutes. Help me plan when to leave.** |
|---|---|
| **Copilot might respond** | Plan to leave by 1:15 PM so you have extra time. |
| **Follow-up prompt** | Add time for lunch before I leave. |
| **Remember** | Check the current bus schedule before leaving. |

## Plan home responsibilities

| **Prompt** | **I need to do laundry, clean my room, and buy groceries before noon. Help me plan my morning.** |
|---|---|
| **Copilot might respond** | Start laundry. Clean the room while the laundry runs. Move the laundry. Buy groceries. |
| **Follow-up prompt** | Add one break. |
| **Remember** | Change the order if another plan works better. |
