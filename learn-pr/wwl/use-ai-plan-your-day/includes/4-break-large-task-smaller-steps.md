A large task can be difficult to start because it includes many smaller jobs. For example, "get ready for work" may include:

- Check the work schedule
- Take a shower
- Brush teeth
- Choose clothes
- Pack lunch
- Check transportation
- Find a work badge
- Leave on time

A person could type:

| **I start work at 9:00 AM. Make a checklist of what I should do before I leave for work, and suggest a time to set my alarm clock.** |
|---|

Copilot can create a checklist that the person can review and change. The person may add something Copilot did not know about, such as taking medication, bringing a uniform, or meeting a ride.

## Include travel time

A schedule should include the time needed to get from one place to another. For example:

| **My class ends at 1:00 PM. I have an appointment at 2:00 PM. The bus ride takes 35 minutes. What time should I leave?** |
|---|

Copilot can use those details to suggest a time. The person should still check the current bus schedule or another trusted source before leaving.

## Include breaks

A plan does not need to fill every minute. Breaks can be added between activities or after a longer task.

| **Help me plan two hours of homework. Add a 15-minute break after the first hour.** |
|---|

Copilot can add the break to the plan. The person can choose a different break time or take a break earlier if needed.

## Change the plan when something changes

Plans often change. The bus may be late. An appointment may move. A task may take longer than expected.

A person can give Copilot the new information:

| **My appointment moved from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Update my plan.** |
|---|

Or:

| **Cleaning took longer than I expected. Move grocery shopping to later.** |
|---|

Copilot can suggest an updated plan. The person checks whether the new plan still works.

## Use a checklist to track progress

A schedule shows when something may happen. A checklist shows what needs to be done.

Someone preparing for school might use a checklist like this:

- Finish the assignment
- Charge the laptop
- Pack the backpack and charging cord
- Put lunch in the refrigerator
- Set an alarm

The person can mark each item when it is finished.
