Think about an event you need to get ready for.

**Examples:**

- A birthday party
- A family visit
- A school event
- A sports practice
- A community activity
- A holiday gathering

## Step 1: Choose an event

What event are you getting ready for?

## Step 2: Write a prompt

Imagine using Copilot. What would you ask?

**Example:** Help me make a checklist for my niece's birthday party on Saturday. I need to buy a gift, wrap it, and be ready to leave by 1:00 PM.

## Step 3: Write a follow-up prompt

After Copilot responds, ask for one small change.

**Example:** Put the checklist in the best order.

## Step 4: Think about the AI response

After AI creates the plan, answer these questions:

- What part of the plan was helpful?
- Did the plan include the important tasks?
- Was anything missing?
- Would you change the order of any tasks?
- Would you use this plan? Why or why not?
