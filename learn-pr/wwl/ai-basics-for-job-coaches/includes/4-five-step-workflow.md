The five-step workflow helps you use AI as part of a thoughtful work process, not as a replacement for judgment. By starting with what you know, writing clear prompts, reviewing and refining the response, and making the final decision yourself, you can create more useful, accurate, and employee-centered materials while maintaining responsibility for the outcome.

1. Start with what you know. Identify the employee's goal, the workplace expectation, and the purpose of the material.
1. Prompt clearly. Use role, task, audience, format, and constraints.
1. Review the answer as you would review a draft from someone who is new to the work.
1. Refine with follow-up requests. Ask for a shorter version, clearer wording, a different format, or a list of unsupported claims.
1. Decide and own the final. Verify the content, involve the employee when appropriate, and use only what is accurate and useful.
