A simple way to improve AI-generated content is to use a clear prompt structure. This five-part prompt framework can help you give AI the context it needs by defining who is speaking, what to create, who it is for, how it should be organized, and any requirements it must follow. The more specific you are in each part, the more useful and relevant the output is likely to be.

| **Part** | **Question** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| **Role** | What perspective should the draft use? | A job coach writing to an employer |
| **Task** | What should AI produce? | Draft a one-page checklist |
| **Audience** | Who will use it? | An adult employee who prefers short sentences |
| **Format** | How should it be organized? | Numbered steps with one idea per line |
| **Constraints** | What must it include or avoid? | Use only the notes provided. No personal details. |
