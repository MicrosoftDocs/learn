Copilot in Fabric in the Data Warehouse workload applies AI to data warehousing tasks. It can generate T-SQL queries, provide code completions, and surface information based on your warehouse schema and metadata.

With features like natural language to SQL, quick actions for fixing and explaining queries, and intelligent suggestions, Copilot helps you interact with your data warehouse more intuitively. Whether you're an experienced data professional or just starting, Copilot can help you work with your data warehouse by simplifying complex tasks.

In this module, you explore key concepts of Microsoft Fabric and Copilot in Data Warehouse to apply its capabilities in data projects.
