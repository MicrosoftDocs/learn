The same skills can support different parts of life. Copilot can help with:

- Creating a study plan
- Organizing homework or coursework
- Making a checklist for work tasks
- Planning household responsibilities
- Keeping track of job search tasks
- Drafting a short message

## Professional language tips

- When writing to a teacher, professor, or supervisor, use words that fit the setting. A work or school message is usually clear, respectful, and focused on the reason for the message. 

- Copilot can help change the tone of a draft. For example, a person can type: Make this message clear and professional for my teacher: [paste the message without private information]. 

- AI can help organize thoughts or draft a message, but it does not know the full situation and cannot take the place of support from another person. For personal concerns or emotional support, talk with someone you trust, such as a family member, friend, teacher, job coach, support professional, or counselor. 

- Read and change the message before sending it. Ask a teacher, supervisor, job coach, or trusted person for help when unsure. 

## Privacy reminder

Schools, workplaces, and programs may have rules about which AI tools can be used and what information can be shared.

- Follow the rules from the school, workplace, or program
- Remove names and private details that are not needed
- Do not share passwords, private health information, grades, student records, or confidential workplace information
- Ask a teacher, supervisor, job coach, or trusted person when unsure
