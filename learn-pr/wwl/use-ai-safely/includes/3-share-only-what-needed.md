## Think before copying, pasting, or uploading

A message, form, photo, or document may include names, account numbers, barcodes, signatures, or other private details. Before sharing it with AI:

- Pause and read the information
- Remove names, numbers, photos, and details that are not needed
- Do not upload an identification card, benefit letter, bank statement, medical form, or other private document
- Follow school, workplace, program, or organization rules
- Ask a trusted person to review it when unsure

## Watch for scams and unsafe requests

Scammers may use calls, texts, emails, social media, websites, or AI-made voices and images to appear real. A message may be a scam even when it uses the name, photo, or voice of someone familiar.

Be careful when a person or message:

- Creates fear, excitement, or pressure to act right away
- Asks for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, a wire transfer, or cash
- Asks for a password, one-time code, account number, or identification number
- Says to keep the request secret
- Sends an unexpected link, attachment, QR code, or phone number
- Claims to be a family member, friend, company, bank, police officer, or government worker and asks for help or payment
- Offers a prize, job, investment, benefit, refund, or urgent account fix that seems too good to be true

> **A clear safety rule:** Do not send money, share private information, or open an unexpected link because of an AI answer or an unexpected message.

## Stop, check, and ask

When something does not feel right, use these three steps:

| **1. STOP** | **Do not reply, pay, share information, select a link, download a file, or call the number in the message.** |
|---|---|
| **2. CHECK** | Contact the person or organization in a different way. Use a phone number, website, app, or contact already known to be real. Do not use the contact information in the unexpected message. |
| **3. ASK** | Tell a trusted person what happened. Ask for help before taking the next step. If money, account access, or private information was already shared, ask for help right away. |
