A short follow-up prompt can ask Copilot to remove details, explain uncertainty, or identify what should be checked. Copilot can help a person slow down and think, but it cannot guarantee that something is safe or real.

### Protect private information

| **Prompt** | **Help me write a message about my appointment without using my home address.** |
|---|---|
| **Copilot might respond** | I have an appointment at 2:00 PM and will need time to travel. |
| **Follow-up prompt** | Remove any private details. |
| **Remember** | Read the message before sharing it. |

### Check an unexpected message

| **Prompt** | **I received a message asking me to pay right away. What warning signs should I look for?** |
|---|---|
| **Copilot might respond** | Copilot may identify pressure, unusual payment methods, requests for private information, or unexpected links as warning signs. |
| **Follow-up prompt** | Give me three steps to check this without replying to the message. |
| **Remember** | Do not send money or share information. Check with a trusted person or contact the organization another way. |

### Check instructions

| **Prompt** | **Explain these work instructions using clear steps: [paste instructions without private details].** |
|---|---|
| **Copilot might respond** | Copilot may create a short list of steps. |
| **Follow-up prompt** | Which parts should I check with my supervisor? |
| **Remember** | Follow current workplace instructions. |
