Imagine you're making a doctor's appointment and you need help planning your day.

## Step 1: Read the prompt

Read this prompt closely, then read it again.

**Example:** _"My name is Sam Jones. I live at 123 Main Street. I have a doctor's appointment at 2:00 PM. What time should I leave?"_

## Step 2: Find information you don't need

Which details could you take out?

- Person's name
- Home address
- Appointment time

## Step 3: Rewrite the prompt

How could you ask the same question without sharing extra private details?

**Example:** _"My appointment is at 2:00 PM. The trip takes 30 minutes. What time should I leave?"_

## Step 4: Think about safe AI use

Answer these questions:

- Which details were private?
- Which details did AI need to answer your question?
- Does the new prompt still give AI enough to help?
- Why is it safer to leave out details you don't need?
- If you weren't sure, who could you ask for help?
