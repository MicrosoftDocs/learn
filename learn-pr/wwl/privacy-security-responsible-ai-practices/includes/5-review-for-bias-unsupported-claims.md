AI drafts can carry assumptions that no one intended. Read the output with these four checks before you use it.

- Remove any assumption that observation or verified information doesn't support.
- Look for lower expectations, deficit-focused wording, patronizing tone, and one-size-fits-all support.
- Verify facts, dates, citations, policy, and local requirements outside the AI response.
- Check that the output focuses on workplace tasks and supports rather than diagnosis or personal traits.
