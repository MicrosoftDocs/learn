Do not use AI:

- To decide whether a person is ready for work or likely to succeed.
- To conduct vocational profiling, discovery, or assessment from keywords.
- To make decisions about disclosure, accommodations, job match, or support without the employee.
- To replace direct observation, conversation, conflict resolution, or relationship-building.
- To translate safety-critical information without competent human verification.
- To process private or organizational information in a tool or manner that is not approved.
