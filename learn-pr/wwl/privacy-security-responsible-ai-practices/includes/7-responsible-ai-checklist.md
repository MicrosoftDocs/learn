Review each flashcard to reinforce the Responsible AI checklist. These questions help ensure your AI use is safe, ethical, and effective in every situation.

| **Check** | **Question** |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | What is AI helping me do, and is AI appropriate for this task? |
| **Approval** | Am I using an approved tool in an approved way? |
| **Privacy** | Am I using only the permitted information needed? |
| **Security** | Am I following rules for storage, access, sharing, and retention? |
| **Consent and transparency** | Does the employee understand and agree to this use when it concerns them? |
| **Accuracy** | Did I verify important information through observation or an authoritative source? |
| **Bias and tone** | Is the output respectful, adult, strengths-based, and free of unsupported assumptions? |
| **Fit** | Does it match the employee, workplace, and local context? |
| **Accountability** | Can I explain what AI did, what I checked, and why I chose the final version? |
