Privacy is about appropriate collection, use, and sharing of information. Security is about protecting information and systems from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. An approved tool may have stronger protections, but approval does not mean every type of information may be entered. Follow your organization's policy, permissions, retention rules, and approved-tool guidance.

> Minimum-information rule: Use only the information needed for the task. When information is not needed, leave it out. Never assume that deleting a chat or file removes every organizational record or system copy.
