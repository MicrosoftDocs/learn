When someone asks which product is "most profitable," you first need to know how "profit" is defined. Some organizations mean _total profit_ (revenue minus cost), while others mean _profit margin_ (profit as a percentage of revenue). Humans resolve that ambiguity through shared business context, and AI needs the same context represented in data to provide valuable results.

BI analysts build reports on semantic models that produce accurate results because they select the intended measures, filters, and visuals. When a business leader asks Copilot the same question directly, the semantic model has to provide that context instead. If the semantic model doesn't make business meaning explicit, Copilot can choose the wrong measure and misdirect decisions.

By preparing your semantic model, you make business concepts, relationships, and calculations clear for AI. **This preparation reduces the need for Copilot to choose between ambiguous measures, technical fields, or competing definitions, making answers more reliable regardless of which AI experience uses the semantic model.**

The goal of this module is to show how preparing data for AI gives Copilot and data agents the business context they need to resolve ambiguity. After completing the module, you can prepare and test a semantic model for reliable AI use and recognize when Fabric IQ adds value beyond that semantic model.
