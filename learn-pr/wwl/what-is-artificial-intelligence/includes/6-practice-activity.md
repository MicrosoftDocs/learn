Think about a task you do regularly at home, work, or school.

Examples:

- Getting ready for work
- Packing a lunch
- Cleaning a room
- Shopping for groceries
- Planning your day

## Step 1: Choose a task

What is one task you would like help with?

## Step 2: Write a prompt

- Imagine you are using Copilot. What would you ask?
- Example: Make a checklist of things I should do before I leave for work.

## Step 3: Think about the AI response

After AI gives you a suggestion, answer these questions:

- What part of the response would be helpful?
- Is there anything you would add or change?
- How would you check that the information is correct?
- Would you use the suggestion? Why or why not?
