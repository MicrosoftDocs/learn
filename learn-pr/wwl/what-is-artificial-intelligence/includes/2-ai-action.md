Someone getting ready for work wants help remembering what to do before leaving. They open Copilot and type:

> I need to leave for work at 8:00 AM. Make a checklist of things to do before I leave.

Copilot might respond:

- Get dressed
- Pack lunch
- Check the bus schedule
- Bring a work badge
- Leave by 8:00 AM
 
The person can use the list, change it, or ask Copilot for a different list.
