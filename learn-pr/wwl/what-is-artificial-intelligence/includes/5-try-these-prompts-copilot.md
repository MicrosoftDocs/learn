These prompts can be copied into Copilot and changed to fit the task. Do not add passwords, a home address, private health information, or other information that should stay private.

| **Use** | **Prompt** |
|---|---|
| **Get organized** | I need to leave for work at 8:00 AM. Make a checklist of things to do before I leave. |
| **Make a list** | I need to buy groceries. Make a shopping list for me. |
| **Plan a task** | Help me plan the steps for cleaning my room. |
| **Create a schedule** | Help me plan my day. I work at 9:00 AM and have an appointment at 2:00 PM. How can I get from work to my appointment on time. |
| **Understand information** | Explain these instructions using simple words: [paste instructions here]. |
| **Get ideas** | Give me three ideas for a healthy lunch to bring to work. [insert some of the foods you have in your fridge and pantry, or plan to get at the grocery store]. |
