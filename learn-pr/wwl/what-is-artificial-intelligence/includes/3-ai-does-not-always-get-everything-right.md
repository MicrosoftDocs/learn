AI can be helpful, but it can make mistakes. Sometimes AI:

- Gets information wrong
- Misses important details
- Gives different answers to the same question
 
It is important to review AI responses before using them.

For example, someone asks Copilot:

> What time does the number 10 bus arrive at my stop tomorrow?

Copilot gives an answer. Before leaving for work, the person should take an extra step and check the official bus schedule. The bus schedule has the most current information.
