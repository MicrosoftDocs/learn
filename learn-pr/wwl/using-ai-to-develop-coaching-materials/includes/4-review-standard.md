Explore each review criterion below to understand what to check and why it matters when finalizing AI-generated coaching materials.

- **Accuracy**: Does the material match direct observation and verified information? Correct anything AI inferred or filled in on its own.
- **Accessibility**: Can the intended reader find, understand, and use the information in the format provided?
- **Respect and adult tone**: Is the language respectful, adult, and person-centered, with no patronizing or deficit-focused wording?
- **Workplace fit**: Does it match the employer's actual process, sequence, and language rather than a generic version of the job?
- **Local fit**: Are laws, program names, and local requirements confirmed with an authoritative local source outside the AI tool?
- **Privacy and security**: Was the tool approved, and did you use only the information permitted by organizational policy?
- **Bias**: Does the output introduce assumptions, lower expectations, or one-size-fits-all support?
- **Employee ownership and voice**: Does the material reflect the employee's own words, choices, and preferences, and have they approved it?

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Confirm employee approval when the material is about them, represents them, or will be shared with others. The profile and the choices it describes belong to the employee.
