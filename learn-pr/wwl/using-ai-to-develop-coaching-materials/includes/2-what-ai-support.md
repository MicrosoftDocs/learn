AI can help with parts of this work, especially drafting, organizing, and adapting information for different audiences. It doesn't replace professional judgment or employee choice. The job coach reviews, refines, and personalizes AI-generated content to make sure it's accurate, relevant, and aligned with the employee's goals and preferences.

| **Material** | **Appropriate AI support** | **People must lead** |
|---|---|---|
| **Task analysis** | Turn verified employer instructions into a numbered draft. | Observe the task, confirm the sequence, and check safety and quality. |
| **Employee-approved profile or summary** | Reformat an existing profile or create an accessible version from content the employee has approved. | Develop the full profile through discovery and partnership. Confirm what the employee agrees to share. Remember that the individual owns the profile. |
| **Prompting and support plan** | Organize a draft from an approved task analysis. | Select the least intrusive support, include coworker supports, and adjust through observation. |
| **Workplace instructions** | Create plain-language or visual versions. | Confirm every safety and quality requirement with the employer. |
| **Practice scenarios** | Draft options for rehearsing likely workplace conversations. | Choose scenarios with the employee and adapt them to the real workplace. |
