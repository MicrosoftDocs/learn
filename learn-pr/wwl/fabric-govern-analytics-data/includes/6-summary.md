Governance protects sensitive data and helps consumers find the right assets. Sensitivity labels make classification visible across the platform and flow through the data pipeline. Endorsement signals which items are tested, certified, or authoritative. Tags, descriptions, and domain assignments make data findable in the OneLake catalog.

The OneLake catalog's Govern section shows the current state of your data estate and surfaces gaps before they cause problems. The Secure section gives a centralized view of who can access what. Together, these tools turn governance from a one-time setup into a visible, maintainable practice.
