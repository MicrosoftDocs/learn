A threat is detected on a workstation in your organization. Your security team needs to establish what happened, how far the activity extends, and what needs attention now.

## Understand the attack and prepare the response

The investigation needs to explain how the activity started, what happened next, and which resources are affected. A sign-in from the workstation to a file server gives investigators a connection to examine. Server activity then needs closer review to establish its role in the incident and whether containment is needed.

Response can proceed on confirmed findings while related activity remains under investigation. Keep important claims tied to their evidence, identify gaps in coverage, and distinguish actions justified now from those that need more information.

Perception's Attack Investigation Agent correlates Microsoft Defender signals around an alert or incident and produces an investigation report. As the investigator, you review the findings and prepare the response handoff, working with the people responsible for approvals and response actions.

## Learning objectives

In this module, you learn how to:

- Define an investigation question and select a suitable alert or incident input.
- Inspect an investigation's progress, evidence, and report.
- Validate the incident verdict and attack timeline against supporting evidence.
- Distinguish affected entities from possible scope and unassessed resources.
- Prepare a prioritized response plan with owners, approval requirements, and completion checks.
