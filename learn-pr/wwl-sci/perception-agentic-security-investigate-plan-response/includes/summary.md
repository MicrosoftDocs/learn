Use investigation findings to explain what happened, establish affected scope, and plan the response. Investigate attack starts from a single alert or incident ID and doesn't require prior triage.

In this module, you learned how to:

- Define an investigation question and select a suitable alert or incident input.
- Inspect an investigation's progress, evidence, and report.
- Validate the incident verdict and attack timeline against supporting evidence.
- Distinguish affected entities from possible scope and unassessed resources.
- Prepare a prioritized response plan with owners, approval requirements, and completion checks.

Keep sound findings even when part of an attack story needs correction. Malicious execution can justify containment while initial access or wider impact remains unresolved. A related entity without available telemetry needs follow-up, not a declaration that it is compromised or safe.

Turn recommendations into actions with specific targets, evidence, owners, and outcome checks. Hand over the supported conclusions, coverage limits, and open decisions. Check response results against the organization's incident-closure criteria rather than treating a completed investigation as a completed response.
