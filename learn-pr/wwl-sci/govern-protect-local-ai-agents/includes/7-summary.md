In this module, you examine how local AI agents expose Contoso and how governance and protection controls reduce that exposure across the agent lifecycle. You can now identify what a developer's local coding assistant can reach, choose controls that fit the risk, and map responsibilities across Microsoft's security and management services.

## What you learned

- You recognize a local agent by where it runs and what it can reach, and you trace its risk to local execution, inherited access, and untrusted input.
- You interpret discovery evidence as showing presence and possible reach, not active use or protection.
- You match discovery, identity, network, runtime inspection, data protection, and containment controls to the risks they address, and you account for the coverage limits of each layer.
- You evaluate detections and responses by recognizing that audit mode records and alerts but allows the action, and by weighing the scope and impact of each response across the lifecycle stages of discover and assess, govern and protect, detect and monitor, investigate, and remediate and recover.
