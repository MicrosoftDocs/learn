In this module, you used Snipping Tool in Windows 11 to turn part of the Contoso project dashboard into a clear visual update. You also saw the specific improvement a Copilot+ PC brings to this familiar workflow: Perfect Screenshot can turn a rough rectangular selection into a tightly framed image, reducing the need for manual cropping.

You learned how to:

- **Choose an appropriate capture type** for the content you want to include.
- **Use Perfect Screenshot on a Copilot+ PC** to automatically adjust a rough selection around prominent on-screen content.
- **Refine a screenshot** with markup that emphasizes important information.
- **Prepare a screenshot for sharing** by using text actions and quick redact, while reviewing the complete image for information that shouldn't be shared.
- **Decide when a screenshot is the right medium** and when sharing source content, text, or a recording would better fit the task.

Use Snipping Tool in Windows 11 when you need a visual snapshot of on-screen information, and use Perfect Screenshot on a Copilot+ PC when you want to quickly create a tightly framed image from a rough rectangular selection.
