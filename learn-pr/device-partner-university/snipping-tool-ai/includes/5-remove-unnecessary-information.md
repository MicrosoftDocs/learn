After you deliver the initial screenshot, your team requests more context for the project update. You decide to share a screenshot of the entire Contoso dashboard to provide it.

However, you realize the full interface also includes project contact information. Your colleagues don't need that information for this update, so the finished screenshot shouldn't include it simply because it happened to be present when you made the capture.

Snipping Tool's **text actions** use text recognition to work with text in a captured image. Among its options, **quick redact** can redact recognized email addresses and phone numbers.

For this exercise, you'll use quick redact to remove the unnecessary contact details before preparing the image for sharing.

**Redact the contact information**

1. Take a screenshot of the entire dashboard interface. Select "Capture" to save the screenshot and open it in the Snipping Tool.
2. With your screenshot open in Snipping Tool, select **text actions**.
:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/snipping-tool-text-point.png" alt-text="Screenshot indicating the location of Snipping Tool text actions.":::

3. Review the text recognized in the screenshot.
:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/snipping-tool-text-selected.png" alt-text="Screenshot showing text actions intelligently selecting text in the image.":::

4. Select **Quick redact** to obscure the email address and phone number.
:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/snipping-tool-text-redaction.png" alt-text="Screenshot showing contact information redacted from the image.":::

5. Review the result.

Confirm that the contact information has been redacted and that the project information you intend to share remains visible.

>[!IMPORTANT]
> Quick redact can help obscure recognized email addresses and phone numbers, but **it isn't a complete privacy or information-security solution**. Before sharing a screenshot, review the entire image for sensitive, confidential, or otherwise unintended information.

Text recognition for Text actions is performed locally on the device.

## Prepare the screenshot for sharing

When you're satisfied with the result, save or share the capture from Snipping Tool. Captured snips are also automatically saved to the Screenshots folder, and **Save as** lets you save another copy.

Before sharing, give the complete image one final review for unintended or sensitive information.

Your Contoso screenshot is now tightly framed, the current milestone is emphasized, and the unnecessary contact information is obscured.
