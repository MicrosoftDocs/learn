Screenshots are a quick way to share on-screen information, but selecting exactly the right area can require careful positioning or cropping afterward. On a Copilot+ PC, Perfect Screenshot improves this familiar Snipping Tool workflow: you can start with a rough rectangular selection, and Snipping Tool can automatically adjust the capture area to tightly frame prominent content.

Suppose you work at a software company called Contoso and need to provide colleagues with a visual update on a website refresh project. You have a project dashboard open on your Copilot+ PC, but you need only part of it for your update. You'll use [Snipping Tool in Windows 11](https://support.microsoft.com/windows/apps/use-snipping-tool-to-capture-screenshots) to choose what to capture, then use Perfect Screenshot on a Copilot+ PC to create a tightly framed image with less manual cropping. You'll also emphasize a key update and remove unnecessary contact information before sharing the result.

Along the way, you'll learn when a screenshot is useful and when another way of sharing information might be more appropriate.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you'll be able to:

- Choose an appropriate method for capturing on-screen content with Snipping Tool.
- Capture focused on-screen content by using Perfect Screenshot on a Copilot+ PC.
- Refine a screenshot so that important information is easy to identify.
- Prepare a screenshot for sharing by removing unnecessary text information.
- Determine when a screenshot is an appropriate way to share on-screen information.

## Prerequisites

- A Windows 11 PC with Snipping Tool is sufficient for the standard screenshot capture, markup, Text actions, and sharing concepts covered in this module.
- A supported Copilot+ PC is required to follow the Perfect Screenshot portion of the exercise.
