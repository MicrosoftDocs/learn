With a standard rectangular capture, getting the framing exactly right can require careful positioning or cropping afterward. Perfect Screenshot on a Copilot+ PC lets you start with a rough rectangular selection and automatically adjusts the capture area to tightly frame prominent on-screen content. This can reduce the need for post-capture cropping.

You'll use Perfect Screenshot to capture the relevant portion of the Contoso project dashboard.

>[!NOTE]
> The Perfect Screenshot experience can look different when you begin a capture from the main Snipping Tool window. Depending on your Snipping Tool and Windows configuration, the capture experience can expose the Perfect Screenshot controls differently. This exercise uses the **Windows logo key + Shift + S** capture overlay for consistency.

## Capture the relevant material

1. Open the Contoso website refresh project dashboard.
:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/dashboard.png" alt-text="Screenshot showing the Contoso project dashboard for a website refresh.":::

2. Press Windows logo key + Shift + S to open the Snipping Tool capture overlay.
3. Select Perfect Screenshot.
:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/perfect-screenshot.png" alt-text="Screenshot indicating the location of the Perfect Screenshot option.":::

4. Drag a rough rectangle around the project information you want to capture.
:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/screenshot-progress.png" alt-text="Screenshot showing a purple selection marquee around a rough part of the image.":::

5. Review the adjusted capture area.
:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/screenshot-complete.png" alt-text="Screenshot showing a smaller, more precise rectangular selection.":::

6. If necessary, drag the handles to fine-tune the boundary.
7. Complete the image capture.

Your initial selection doesn't need to be exact. Perfect Screenshot analyzes the visible content and adjusts the boundary around prominent content, helping reduce manual cropping afterward.

You now have a screenshot that focuses on the project information your colleagues need rather than everything that happened to be visible on your display.

>[!NOTE]
> Perfect Screenshot applies to image captures on supported Copilot+ PCs. It automatically adjusts the selected capture area to tightly frame prominent on-screen content, reducing the need for post-capture cropping.

## Think beyond the scenario

Perfect Screenshot adds the most value when you need part of the screen rather than an entire window or display, and you don't want to precisely position and crop the selection yourself.
