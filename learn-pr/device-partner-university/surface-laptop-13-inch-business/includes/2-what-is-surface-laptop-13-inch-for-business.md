Surface Laptop 13-inch is a series of Copilot+ PC laptops built by Microsoft designed specifically for business users. Copilot+ PCs are a category of Windows PCs that combine powerful AI-enabled hardware with experiences designed for productivity, creativity, and efficiency. The Surface Laptop 13-inch series support mobile work through hardware, software that supports adaptable workflows, and security that protects important data.

>[!NOTE]
> Copilot+ PC features are available only on select configurations with 16 GB of RAM or more; additional system requirements may apply. See more at [Windows 11 Specs and System Requirements](https://aka.ms/copilotpluspcspro).

## Hardware

Modern professionals frequently move between home offices, customer sites, meeting rooms, and travel. They need a device that stays responsive throughout the day, supports emerging AI workloads, and remains portable enough to work anywhere. Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch addresses these needs through Copilot+ PC performance, long battery life, and a lightweight design. Let's explore some of the device specifications that solve these needs.

There are three different versions of Surface Laptop 13-inch:

- Surface Laptop for Business, 13-inch (1st Edition) (Qualcomm)
- Surface Laptop for Business, 13-inch (1st Edition) (Intel)
- Surface Laptop for Business, 13-inch (2nd Edition) (Qualcomm)

While these devices share many similarities, there are a few differences to note between them:

**Performance:** All versions of Surface Laptop 13-inch feature powerful processors that are optimized for AI experiences that define modern workflows. All three versions feature CPUs (Central Processing Units) that are built to deliver fast performance anywhere with efficient multitasking.

AI is becoming part of everyday work, whether employees are searching for information, generating content, analyzing data, or collaborating with Copilot. The dedicated NPU helps these AI experiences run efficiently on the device, enabling responsive performance while helping preserve battery life.

**Display:** All variants of Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch feature a 13-inch PixelSense™ touchscreen display with a resolution of 1920 x 1280 (178 pixels per inch). The display features a 3:2 aspect ratio, which is slightly taller than a standard 16:9 widescreen display—offering business users more vertical real estate, excellent for reading emails in Outlook or editing documents in Microsoft Word. The displays on Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch are also anti-reflective and ISO 9241-307 certified—enhancing visibility, reducing glare, and improving comfort for long working sessions, perfect for getting work done at the office or out in the field. **Ports and charging:** Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch features two USB-C®/USB 3.2 ports on the right side of the device. These ports support charging, data transfer, Surface Thunderbolt™ 4 Dock, and other accessories. For display expansion, it supports DisplayPort 1.4a with support for up to two 4-K monitors at 60 Hz refresh rate. On the left side of the device is a USB-A 3.2 port for peripherals such as mice, keyboards, and portable hard drives.

**Connectivity:** To keep up with modern hybrid business needs, Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch is equipped with updated connectivity technology. It features Wi-Fi 7, offering stable internet speeds, lower latency, and higher network capacity—ensuring that business users can access their most important documents wherever their work takes them. Bluetooth® Core 5.4 technology is also included, enabling fast and reliable connections to essential accessories like wireless keyboards and mice.

**Key takeaway:** Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch gives business users the hardware foundation they need to stay productive wherever work happens, combining AI-ready performance, a portable touchscreen display, reliable connectivity, and flexible ports that support modern workflows without adding complexity.

## Security

Security shouldn't require employees to change how they work. Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch helps organizations protect identities, devices, and data while maintaining a simple user experience. From sign-in to threat protection, security is built into the device from the moment it powers on.

- **Secured-core PC protection:** Windows 11 Secured-core PCs are designed to provide protection across hardware, firmware, and the operating system. For business users, this helps reduce exposure to modern threats while giving IT teams a secure foundation that is enabled out of the box.
- **Microsoft Pluton:** The Microsoft Pluton security processor integrates protection directly into the device silicon and works with enterprise-grade TPM 2.0 and BitLocker support. This helps protect sensitive business data, secure device identity, and support encryption policies without requiring extra hardware.
- **Windows Hello for Business:** Windows Hello fingerprint recognition with Enhanced Sign-in Security makes it easier for users to sign in quickly without entering a password. This improves the user experience while helping organizations reduce password-related risks, especially for employees working in shared or public environments.
- **Continuous protection:** Continuous protection helps Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch support secure productivity throughout the device experience. PC Manager helps optimize system performance, Windows 11 AI contributes to app security, and Microsoft Defender provides real-time threat monitoring and identity protection—safeguarding data while work continues.

**Key Takeaway:** Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch combines Windows security features and Microsoft hardware protection to help safeguard business data without adding complexity for users. Organizations benefit from stronger protection, simplified management, and support for secure work across various environments.
