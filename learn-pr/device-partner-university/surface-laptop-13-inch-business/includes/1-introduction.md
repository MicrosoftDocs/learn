For modern business users, devices must blend performance to handle their workflows, portability to perform where work requires, and security to trust their most important data from day one. In this module, you'll learn more about Surface Laptop 13-inch, a collection of laptops made by Microsoft designed for mobility and AI-powered productivity.

>[!NOTE]
> This module explores the commercial-focused versions of Surface Laptop for Business, 13-inch. Many of these features are also available in a version designed for consumers. To learn more about the consumer versions of Microsoft Surface Laptop for Business, visit the Surface product page [here](https://www.microsoft.com/surface).

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you'll be able to:

- Describe how Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch supports mobile productivity, AI-enhanced workflows, and secure business use.
- Identify key hardware features, including processor options, display capabilities, ports, connectivity, and battery life.
- Explain how Copilot Voice, Copilot Vision, and Improved Windows Search help users interact with content and find information more efficiently.
- Describe how built-in security features, including Windows Hello, TPM 2.0, Microsoft Pluton, and Microsoft Defender offer device and data protection.
- Determine how Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch can support knowledge workers and education users across common work and learning scenarios.
