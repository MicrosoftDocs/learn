Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch is a great solution for a wide range of users but there are two specific use cases that it's ideal for: knowledge workers and education users. Let's explore how Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch helps these users accomplish their goals and remain productive.

## Knowledge workers

Knowledge workers such as marketers, consultants, project managers, account managers, financial analysts, and hybrid employees often need a device that can move seamlessly between home, office, and customer sites.

Imagine you're an account manager who starts your day reviewing customer proposals from home, joining Teams meetings while traveling, and finishing presentations at a client site. Throughout the day, you uses Improved Windows Search to locate files, Copilot Vision to review presentation content, and Windows Studio Effects to present professionally in virtual meetings. Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch provides the performance, battery life, and security you need without being tied to a desk.

You  have the following needs:

- Performance for multitasking and AI-powered workflows.
- Long battery life for work throughout the day.
- Portability for hybrid and mobile work.
- Reliable connectivity.
- High-quality video conferencing capabilities.
- Strong security for business data.

Separate from the users, device decision makers also have needs to consider:

- Easy deployment and management of devices.
- Built-in security.
- Durable, business-ready hardware.
- AI-ready devices that support future workflows.
- Lower total cost of ownership.
- Compatibility with Microsoft 365 and modern management tools.

Knowledge workers have the following key use cases to consider:

- Working across Microsoft 365 apps such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams.
- Participating in hybrid meetings with the Surface Studio Camera and Windows Studio Effects.
- Using Copilot, Copilot Voice, and Copilot Vision to improve productivity.
- Collaborating across office, home, and customer locations.
- Accessing files quickly through Improved Windows Search.

**Key takeaway:** For these users, Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch combines AI-ready performance, a lightweight design, long battery life, and built-in security features that help professionals stay productive wherever work happens. The touchscreen display, modern connectivity, and support for Copilot+ PC experiences enable employees to work more efficiently while maintaining a secure and manageable computing experience.

## Education users

Education users such as higher education students, faculty members, researchers, and academic staff need devices that support learning, collaboration, and productivity across classrooms, campuses, and remote environments.

These users have the following needs:

- Portability for learning on the go.
- Reliable performance for coursework and research.
- Long battery life throughout the day.
- Easy access to files and resources.
- Secure sign-in and data protection.
- Flexible ways to interact with content.

Separate from the users, device decision makers also have needs to consider:

- Manageability across large deployments.
- Device security and data protection.
- Durability for everyday use.
- Support for AI-enhanced learning experiences.
- Lower support and maintenance requirements.
- Integration with Microsoft education and productivity tools.

Education users have the following key use cases to consider:

- Completing coursework and assignments across multiple locations.
- Conducting research and managing large numbers of documents.
- Collaborating in Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365 applications.
- Using Improved Windows Search to quickly locate files and notes.
- Using Copilot Voice and Copilot Vision to better understand content and improve productivity.

**Key takeaway:** Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch delivers the portability, performance, and security needed for modern educational experiences. Students and educators benefit from a versatile touchscreen device that supports AI-powered productivity, flexible input methods, and secure access to coursework and institutional resources. The combination of portability, battery life, search capabilities, and built-in security help users stay productive throughout the school day and beyond.
