## Copilot Voice and Vision

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/copilot-voice.png" alt-text="Screenshot of Copilot Voice interface on Surface Laptop for Business, 13-inch.":::

Employees often spend time switching between applications, searching for information, or trying to understand unfamiliar content. Copilot Voice and Copilot Vision help reduce these interruptions by providing conversational assistance and contextual guidance directly within the workflow.

In addition to input via touchscreen, keyboard, and touchpad that we explored earlier, Copilot Vision and Copilot Voice utilize contextual screen reading and voice input to accomplish tasks and offer insights to keep you more productive.

Let's start with Copilot Voice. Copilot Voice uses advanced speech recognition and natural language processing to understand your spoken words and engage in conversation with you. When you start a Copilot Voice conversation, Copilot begins listening and processing your voice input. Copilot generates responses and will speak the response out loud. Once the conversation has ended, a text transcript of the conversation is made available.

Let's explore how to use Copilot Voice:

1. You can initiate a Copilot Voice conversation in the Copilot app by pressing the microphone icon in the composer.

>[!NOTE]
> If it's your first time using Copilot Voice, you may need to grant microphone access to Copilot on your Surface. If you change your mind later, you can turn off Copilot access to your microphone in system settings.

2. Once the microphone is enabled, Copilot will play an audio greeting, and you can have a voice conversation with Copilot.
3. You can mute yourself by clicking on the microphone icon, which will disable the microphone and stop Copilot from listening. Selecting the microphone icon again unmutes, allowing you to resume the conversation.
4. To end a voice conversation, select the X icon. Copilot Voice will disengage and stop listening. Voice conversations may also be time-limited and will automatically disconnect once you reach the time limit.

>[!IMPORTANT]
> Voice data is used to provide the Copilot Voice service. You can also control whether your Copilot Voice conversations are used for model training. Text transcripts produced from your Copilot Voice conversations are handled in the same way as conversation history from your other Copilot interactions.

Next, let's explore Copilot Vision. Using AI, Copilot Vision can scan, analyze, and offer insights based on what Copilot sees on your display. When you start a Copilot Vision session, Copilot can view the screen, app, or page you choose to share, then use Copilot Voice to guide you through what you are seeing.

>[!NOTE]
> Copilot Vision doesn't use data from your Vision session to train our models. Session data may be temporarily retained for up to 47 hours to support user-submitted feedback and is automatically deleted afterward if no feedback is submitted. A transcript of your conversation with Vision is saved in your chat history so you can revisit it later. Your conversation history isn't used for training or personalization, and you can delete it at any time.

You can use Copilot Vision in Windows and in Microsoft Edge. Follow these steps for Windows use:

1. Start a Voice call in Copilot. Vision is off by default.
2. To add Vision to the Voice session, select **Share screen**.
3. Select the screen or app you want to share with Copilot. While Copilot Vision is active, you'll see an outline around the shared screen or app. You can share up to two apps at a time.
4. Make sure your microphone and audio are enabled. Copilot Vision uses voice input and output to answer questions about the shared content.
5. A floating toolbar appears with Vision and Voice controls. Copilot will greet you, and you can begin asking questions about the shared content.
6. To mute yourself, select the microphone icon. This stops Copilot from listening. Select the microphone icon again to resume.
7. To end the Vision session, select Stop in the floating toolbar. When the session ends, Copilot immediately stops observing the shared content.

Copilot Vision works with most sites and web pages. Follow these steps to use it in Edge:

1. Sign in to Edge with your Microsoft account.
2. Select the Copilot icon in the Edge toolbar.
3. Select the Voice (waves) button.
4. Start a Voice session with Copilot.
5. Select Share screen to add Vision to the Voice session.
6. Go to any supported website, video, or PDF you want to ask Copilot about. When you're using Vision, a toolbar in the Copilot side pane lets you know your Vision session is active. Vision stays with you as you browse from page to page until you're ready to end the session.
7. Ask Copilot questions about the page or content you're viewing.
8. To stop the session, select X in the toolbar at the bottom of the Copilot windows, close the Copilot side pane, or exit Edge.

**Key takeaway:** Copilot Voice and Copilot Vision give users more flexible ways to interact with their Surface Laptop by combining spoken prompts with screen-aware assistance. Together, these experiences help users ask questions, understand shared content, and stay in the flow of work while maintaining control over what Copilot can hear or see.

## Improved Windows Search

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/improved-windows-search.png" alt-text="Screenshot of improved Windows Search interface in File Explorer on Surface Laptop for Business, 13-inch.":::

On Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch, finding files is simplified, ensuring that time isn't wasted looking for the files that you need. By using natural language, you can find the files and settings that you need without needing to remember the full file name. This feature uses on-device AI to scan your files and find what you're looking for quickly and efficiently.

Improved Windows Search is built into the following places on your Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch:

- File Explorer
- Windows Search box in the taskbar
- Settings app

To initiate a search, simply select the search box and begin typing what you're looking for. Because it uses AI to understand your intent, it doesn't require exact file name matches, perfect phrasing, or even correct spelling. For example, if you were looking for a file was named "biology_research_spring.pptx," you could type something as simple as "science deck" and it would surface the file.

Improved Windows Search will index and support your searches automatically. You can manage what is indexed and included using the following steps:

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Privacy & Security**.
3. Select **Searching Windows** to adjust which locations are indexed on your Surface.
4. Turn on **Enhanced** to index your entire PC.

>[!NOTE]
> It's recommended that you plug in your Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch for the initial search indexing to get completed.

This feature is optimized for six languages:

- Chinese (simplified)
- English
- French
- German
- Japanese
- Spanish

Improved Windows Search supports all the usual file types for documents (.txt, .pdf, .docx, .doc, .rtf, .pptx, .ppt, .xls, .xlsx) and images (.jpg/.jpeg, .png, .gif, .bmp, .ico)

**Key takeaway:** Improved Windows Search helps users find files and settings faster by using natural language and on-device AI, so they don't need to remember exact file names, perfect wording, or precise spelling. By surfacing relevant results across File Explorer, the taskbar search box, and Settings, it reduces time spent searching and helps users stay focused on their work.

## Secure out of the box

Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch offers business users security from chip-to-cloud. This security model works out of the box and spans across multiple layers of hardware, firmware, software, application, cloud, and identity. Let's explore the layers of this security stack:

- **Hardware:** Data is encrypted and protected in a sandboxed environment that stores passwords, PIN numbers, and certificates with Trusted Platform Module 2.0 (TPM 2.0).
- **Firmware:** Automated updates to Microsoft firmware make startup faster and more secure, while Device Firmware Configuration Interface (DFCI) enables remote management of hardware components—such as camera and Bluetooth.
- **Operating system:** With advanced Windows operating system security features enabled by default, your Surface Laptop is proactively protected from threats.
- **Cloud:** Always-on security features keep data, device, and identity more secure.

Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch also enables password-less security with the built-in Windows Hello fingerprint reader. This allows business users to add another security layer to keep their data protected. Let's explore how to set up this feature:

1. In the Settings app, select **Accounts > Sign-in options**.
2. Under **Ways to sign in**, select **Fingerprint recognition (Windows Hello)**.
3. Select **Set up**.
4. Place your finger on the fingerprint sensor on your keyboard. You can select one or multiple fingers to be read by the scanner.
5. You'll get a notification when the setup is complete.
6. You can now use the fingerprint sensor to unlock your Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch.

**Key takeaway:** Surface Laptop for Business 13-inch helps protect users from the first startup with chip-to-cloud security across hardware, firmware, Windows, cloud, and identity. Built-in protections like TPM 2.0, secure firmware management, default Windows security features, and Windows Hello fingerprint sign-in help organizations safeguard data while giving users a simple, passwordless way to work securely.
