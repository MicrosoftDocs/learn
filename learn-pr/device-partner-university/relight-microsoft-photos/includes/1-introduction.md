Say you have an important customer-facing event coming up. It could be a conference, webinar, customer presentation, or industry networking event. As you prepare, you decide to update your professional photo so that your Teams profile, company directory, and professional networks present a polished and professional image.

You find a photo that captures the right expression and composition, but the lighting isn't ideal. Shadows make some facial features difficult to see, and the image doesn't have the polished look you're aiming for.

You could spend time trying to recreate the ideal conditions to take a better-lit version of the same photo, with uncertain results. Instead, you use Relight in Microsoft Photos on a Copilot+ PC to improve the lighting and create a more professional-looking portrait right on your device.

In this module, you learn how Relight can help you enhance portrait photos by adjusting lighting sources while maintaining a natural appearance.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you can:

- Describe how Relight enhances portrait photos.
- Identify situations where Relight can improve professional images.
- Adjust virtual lighting by using Relight in Microsoft Photos.
- Apply best practices to create a natural-looking professional portrait.
