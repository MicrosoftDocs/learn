## Why lighting matters in professional portraits

A portrait is often the first way your customers, partners, and colleagues see you. Good lighting can help create a strong first impression by making your features easier to identify and preventing distracting elements from making it into the frame.

Well-balanced lighting can:

- Improve visibility and clarity.
- Help the subject stand out from the background.
- Create a polished, professional appearance.
- Enhance consistency across business profiles and communications.

Even when a photo has good composition and image quality, uneven lighting can reduce its overall impact.

## What is Relight?

[Relight is a feature available in Microsoft Photos](https://support.microsoft.com/windows/ai/ai-apps/relight-in-photos) on Copilot+ PCs that allows you to enhance photos by adjusting virtual light sources after a picture has been taken.

Instead of retaking the photo or using expert photo-editing techniques, you can reposition light, adjust brightness, and refine the overall appearance of a portrait. This makes it easier to improve photos that could benefit from better lighting but are otherwise strong candidates for professional use.

In our scenario, Relight can help transform a well-composed but unevenly lit photo into a more polished profile image.

Relight also demonstrates a broader advantage of Copilot+ PCs. Rather than requiring specialized editing skills, additional software, or cloud-based services, Copilot+ PCs can use local AI capabilities to help you complete common tasks more quickly.

In this case, that means improving a professional photo. In other scenarios, it might mean enhancing creativity, streamlining communication, or helping you work more efficiently. Relight is one example of how Copilot+ PC experiences can help reduce effort while improving outcomes.

>[!NOTE]
> Relight is enabled by the local AI capabilities on Copilot+ PCs. These capabilities allow Microsoft Photos to analyze images and provide advanced editing experiences locally.
> As a result, you can experiment with lighting adjustments and quickly see how those changes affect your photo, without sending your data to external services or servers.

## When Relight is most useful

Relight works especially well when:

- Your face appears more shadowed than intended.
- Lighting falls unevenly across the subject.
- A strong photo needs minor refinements before being shared.
- You want to improve a professional portrait without taking a new picture.

In short, Relight can help you refine existing photos to achieve a more balanced and professional look.
