Ink Canvas features various tools and interactions that can help your notetaking experience become more efficient. Let's explore some of these features and how to use them.

## Create a shape with Draw and Hold

Ink Canvas features a Draw and Hold gesture that can turn hand-drawn lines and shapes into polished shapes in the application. To use this feature, do the following:

1. Using your pen, draw a line or shape with a single stroke.
2. When you finish drawing, keep the tip of your pen pressed against the screen briefly.
3. Ink Canvas automatically converts your drawing into a precise shape.
4. Keep holding the pen against the screen and drag to resize or rotate the converted shape.
5. Lift your pen to finish.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/draw-and-hold.gif" alt-text="Animation showing shape creation using the Draw and Hold tool in Ink Canvas on a Surface device.":::

Draw and Hold supports the creation of the following shapes:

- Arrows
- Circles
- Ellipses
- Hexagons
- Lines
- Pentagons
- Rectangles
- Rhombuses
- Squares
- Trapezoids
- Triangles

## Use the toolbar in Ink Canvas

The Ink Canvas toolbar gives you quick access to tools for selecting content, writing, highlighting, erasing, adding images, and undoing or redoing actions. The toolbar features various selection tools, pen types, eraser, and touch elements. Let's explore these in more detail.

### Select

Use **Select** to drag a rectangular selection around ink, images, and other content on the canvas. After you select content, you can move, resize, copy, or delete it.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/toolbar-select.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the select button in the Ink Canvas toolbar on a Surface device.":::

### Lasso

Use **Lasso** to draw a free-form loop around ink, images, or a combination of content. You can move the selected content or choose an available action from the selection menu.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/lasso-select.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the lasso tool in the Ink Canvas toolbar on a Surface device.":::

### Pen types

- **Ink pen:** Use the **Ink pen** for writing or sketching with a consistent stroke width
- **Fountain pen:** Use the **Fountain pen** to create expressive strokes. The line thickness changes based on the speed and pressure of your stroke.
- **Brush pen:** Use the **Brush pen** to create artistic strokes and lettering. The line thickness changes based on the angle of your stroke.

You can also change pen thickness and color. Select the active pen to open its options. Choose a thickness and color, select a recently used color, or create a custom color.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/pen-types.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the pen type select tool in Ink Canvas on a Surface device allowing for color and thickness adjustment.":::

### Highlight content

Use the **Highlighter** to emphasize ink or other content on the canvas. Select the active **Highlighter** to choose from the available thickness and colors.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/highlight-content.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the highlighter menu within Ink Canvas on a Surface device showing a color picker and thickness adjustment tool.":::

### Erase ink

Use the **Eraser** to remove an entire ink stroke from the canvas.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/erase-ink.png" alt-text="Screenshot showing the Erase ink button on the toolbar in Ink Canvas on a Surface device.":::

### Draw with touch

Turn on **Draw with touch** to draw with your finger on a touch-enabled screen. Use two fingers to pan or zoom the canvas.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/draw-touch.png" alt-text="Screenshot showing the Draw with touch button on the Ink Canvas toolbar on a Surface device.":::

### Add an image

Select **Image** to choose a picture to add to your canvas. Depending on the options available on your device, you can upload an image from your device, take a photo, or choose a stock image. After you add the image, use **Select** to move or resize it.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/add-image.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the Upload from device button on Ink Canvas on a Surface device.":::

### Undo or redo an action

Select **Undo** to reverse your most recent action. Select **Redo** to restore an action that you undid.
