## Pen-first design built around Surface Slim Pen

The Surface Slim Pen is designed to create a natural writing feel on Surface devices—featuring a sharper pen tip, tilt support, and haptic feedback. With 4,096 pressure levels, the experience attempts to mirror that feeling of writing using a traditional pen and paper.

Slim Pen also features palm rejection, a feature that helps to reduce accidental touch triggers when resting your hand on the screen while using the pen. This experience all comes together with zero-force inking on Slim Pen, which allows ink to appear on the screen as soon as the pen hits the display.

Ink Canvas utilizes these features by making pen the primary input as opposed to being an auxiliary input.  Because Ink Canvas is designed to take advantage of pen input, having the necessary pen hardware that creates a more natural writing experience leads to a more natural outcome. With Surface Slim Pen on Surface devices, business users can ideate without worrying about the way they're using the pen and then use Ink Canvas' capabilities to create content.

## AI workflow experiences

Ink Canvas allows users to combine handwriting, sketches, annotations, and visual references as input for AI generation. Consider a user looking to create a mockup of a diagram they're putting together for a presentation. Using Ink Canvas, they draw the diagram, make annotations, and incorporate reference images. Together, these can look messy, but with Ink Canvas they can leverage AI to create something to be used in the presentation, such as a visualization or diagram.

This is enhanced on Surface because of screen technology that's optimized for pen interactions—featuring hardware digitizer technology and software calibration settings that enhance pen precision, pressure sensitivity, and touch blocking. This is in addition to specific hardware advantages in Surface Slim Pen that make writing more natural.

For the user, this means a more accurate representation of their vision in their sketches, which leads to the ability to add more detail. This part is crucial, as more detail provides more context for the capabilities in Ink Canvas—leading to a more accurate representation of your vision to use in your presentation.

## The beginning of an AI workflow

AI is rapidly transforming the way we work, but it can also lead to depending on a variety of different tools. With the hardware and software ecosystem of Surface, Windows, and Microsoft 365, Ink Canvas becomes a part of the productivity stack on Surface.

Imagine that you're an engineer for an automobile manufacturer. You have considered a new design idea for a part and you are drafting a proposal to integrate it as part of the manufacturing process. Using Ink Canvas, Surface, and Surface Slim Pen you're able to turn your idea into a rough sketch and then into a proposal:

1. As you gather your thoughts of what this new design would look like, you click on the top of your Surface Slim Pen. This opens Ink Canvas immediately and you're able to start drawing your diagram.
2. You sketch what you envision, start annotating finer details, and bring in reference images to represent what you are hoping the part will look like visually. When you're done, all the details are there but it is not easily legible to someone not intimately familiar with the design process.
3. Using the built-in AI tools in Ink Canvas, you're able to turn sketches and annotations into a prototype mockup. This will be used in your project proposal.
4. Using this final product, you import this canvas project into Microsoft 365 Copilot. With this project file as a reference, you're able to draft the outline of what should be included in the project proposal.
5. Copilot helps you create a Word file and you type out your proposal, listing the various efficiencies and benefits of this new design to your organization. But upon further inspection, you notice that it features too much technical specification that may not be understood by decision-makers.
6. Using Copilot in Word, you can revise the proposal with simpler language, drawing on the document's content to help explain technical details to stakeholders.
7. Finally, you draft an email in Outlook to the stakeholders and schedule a meeting on Microsoft Teams to discuss next steps.

This process was completed as a combined solution with the integration of Surface hardware, Windows software, and Microsoft 365 AI capabilities to assist in the completion of a project. Because all of the files and identities are connected within your organization, friction was reduced—enabling you to draft this proposal efficiently.
