## Retail store compliance

**Compliance challenge**: Contoso is a fictional national retailer that relies on standardized planograms to ensure products are displayed consistently across every store. Store associates and field managers must verify that shelves, displays, and promotional materials match the approved layout.

**How local AI helps**: Using a **Copilot+ PC**, an employee can take a photo of a store display and use local AI to analyze the image directly on the device. The image classification model identifies products, fixtures, and display elements, then compares what it detects against the expected planogram.

**Business value**: Because the AI runs locally on the device, employees can identify correctly placed, missing, misplaced, or low-stock items with supported local models and APIs, reducing reliance on cloud compute for eligible workloads. This helps support more consistent inspection workflows, reduce manual inspections, and supports consistent customer experience across locations.

### Why this matters

This scenario shows how **image classification and local AI** can help retailers automate visual inspections and improve operational consistency. Running the model directly on a **Copilot+ PC** enables faster decision-making, offline productivity, and reduced reliance on cloud connectivity.

By bringing AI-powered image analysis to the endpoint, organizations can turn routine inspection workflows into responsive, device-powered experiences taking advantage of Copilot+ PCs ability to provide **unmetered intelligence**.

## Sales proposal assistant

**Sales productivity challenge**: Contoso’s fictional sales team spends significant time creating follow-up emails, account summaries, and proposal drafts for customers. When sellers are traveling or working in locations with limited connectivity, they still need reliable access to AI-powered assistance to maintain their workflows and productivity for successful business outcomes.

**How local AI helps**: Using a **Copilot+ PC** and local AI capabilities, a seller can enter notes from a customer conversation and use a text generation model to create a professional follow-up email, meeting summary, or first draft of a proposal. Because the model runs on the device, the seller can generate content with supported local models and APIs, reducing reliance on cloud compute for eligible workloads.

**Business value**: This helps sellers stay productive wherever they work while keeping communications clear, consistent, and customer-ready. By combining local AI with Copilot+ PCs, organizations can bring intelligent productivity experiences directly to the endpoint, reduce reliance on cloud connectivity, and improve responsiveness.

### Why this matters

This scenario shows how **text generation and local AI** can help sellers move faster by turning customer notes into clear, useful business content. Running the model directly on a **Copilot+ PC** supports productivity in more places, reduces reliance on cloud connectivity, and reinforces Microsoft’s vision of **Unmetered Intelligence**.
