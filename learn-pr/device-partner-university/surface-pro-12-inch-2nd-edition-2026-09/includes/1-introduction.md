Work today is evolving and business users' preferences are changing. A CIRP (Cooperative Institutional Research Program) survey found that 30% of tablet owners are using their tablet for work or school-related productivity tasks. For organizations, this trend reflects growing demand for devices that support productivity in a highly mobile form factor. When users are looking for a new laptop, over 60% have indicated that they would prefer a device that is both portable and powerful enough to replace a traditional laptop.

In this module, you'll learn about Microsoft Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition)—a Copilot+ PC built by Microsoft that supports the adaptability, portability, and performance needs of modern business users to help them get work done from everywhere.

>[!NOTE]
> This module explores the commercial-focused version of Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition). Many of these features are also available in a version designed for consumers. To learn more about the consumer version of Microsoft Surface Pro for Business (12-inch), visit the Surface product page [here](https://www.microsoft.com/surface).

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you'll be able to:

- Describe how Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) supports portable, flexible work experiences for modern business users.
- Identify key hardware features of Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition), including performance, display, connectivity, ports, and input methods.
- Explain how Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) enables productivity with touch, pen, keyboard, Canvas, Copilot, and Windows 11 tablet experiences.
- Set up and use essential Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) features, including Windows Hello, OneDrive backup, Surface Slim Pen, and Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard for Business.
- Evaluate when Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is a good fit for frontline, education, and mobile work scenarios.
