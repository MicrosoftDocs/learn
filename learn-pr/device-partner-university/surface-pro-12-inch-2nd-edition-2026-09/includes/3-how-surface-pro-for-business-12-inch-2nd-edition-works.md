Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is a Copilot+ PC that's ideal for mobile work use cases and business-centric portability. Let's get familiar with the device's hardware before you turn it on for the first time.

## Ports and powering on

Let's start on the right side of the device. You'll see two USB-C® ports. These ports can support charging in addition to connecting peripherals—such as monitors, portable hard drives, and docking stations. When first booting up Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition), it's recommended to run through the initial setup with the device plugged in. On the top left corner of the device, you'll see a power button that serves the dual purpose of powering on the device and locking the screen while powered on.

>[!NOTE]
> Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) supports fast charging with a minimum 60 W charger via USB-C.

On the front of the device, you'll see the Full HD front-facing Surface Studio Camera with 1080p resolution. This camera has support for Windows Studio Effects with automatic framing, portrait blur, creative filters, eye contact, and background blur. This will be the primary way that you capture video for virtual meetings through apps such as Microsoft Teams.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/windows-setup.gif" alt-text="Animation showing the Windows setup process on Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition).":::

Next to the Surface Studio Camera is the Windows Hello face authentication camera with Enhanced Sign-in Security. When set up, your Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) will scan your face and securely sign into your device when you press the power button.

Once you power on your Surface, you can quickly set it up and start working. Start by selecting your region and language, then connecting to your local network. Once the basics are set up, sign in to your organization account. Doing this allows you to synchronize files and data across devices. Logging in to your account will also automatically sign you into native applications like Microsoft Edge.

After your account is synchronized, you'll have the option to set up biometric unlocking through Windows Hello to keep your device secure. Then import settings from previous devices with your Microsoft Account backup, and finally set up privacy settings. And now your Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is ready to be used.

**Key takeaway:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is designed to get users productive quickly with modern connectivity, simplified setup, and integrated collaboration features. Users can connect essential accessories, securely sign in, and start working with minimal configuration.

## Using Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard for Business

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/surface-pro-12-inch-keyboard.jpeg" alt-text="Photo of Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard for Business designed for Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition).":::

Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard for Business (sold separately) transforms your Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) from a tablet into a full Windows laptop experience. It features a matte palm rest, keyboard lighting, precision touchpad, and a 360-degree hinge ensuring a comfortable typing experience for long work sessions in various locations. Let's learn how to connect it to your Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition).

Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard features five magnets at the top of the keyboard that connect to the bottom of your Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition). Bring the keyboard close to the device, and the magnets snap into place automatically. To detach the keyboard, rotate the Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard for Business away from Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition).

It's important to keep your Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard updated to ensure optimal performance. Updates can be installed from the Surface app; here's how to check for updates to ensure that your keyboard is running on the latest firmware version:

- With your keyboard connected, select Start, search for Surface, then select the app from the list of results.
- From the choice of connected accessories at the bottom of the app, select your keyboard.
- Expand Firmware Update to view the currently installed firmware and see whether it's up to date. If needed, select the option to update the device.
- After installing the update, disconnect Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) from the keyboard and then reconnect it.

Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard features a precision touchpad that uses two buttons, which can be used as a mouse—and gestures to interact with your Surface. Let's review some of those gestures and what they can do:

| **Gesture** | **Action** |
| :---: | :---: |
| Press down on the left side of the touchpad | Click or select an item |
| Press down on the right side of the touchpad | Open commands in Windows apps or a context menu in desktop apps |
| Swipe three fingers on the touchpad | Display virtual desktop |
| Slide two fingers horizontally or vertically | Scroll through a screen or document |
| Pinch your thumb and forefinger together or move them apart | Zoom in or out |

You can adjust touchpad settings to customize gestures, adjust cursor speed, and scrolling direction. To access these settings, go to **Start > Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad**.

**Kay takeaway:** The Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard helps transform Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) from a tablet into a full laptop experience, giving users the flexibility to work comfortably in various locations and workstyles.
