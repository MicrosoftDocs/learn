Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) presents a compelling solution for these users, providing a device that moves with them throughout the workday. **Key takeaway:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) provides frontline employees with a portable device that supports mobility, connectivity, and security while helping organizations simplify deployment, management, and long-term device maintenance.
