Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is a Copilot+ PC built by Microsoft that is designed specifically for business users. Copilot+ PCs are a category of Windows PCs that combine powerful AI-enabled hardware with experiences designed to improve productivity, creativity, and efficiency. With a focus on portability, Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) offers a Windows laptop experience in a compact tablet form factor. For business users that work from multiple locations, Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) offers flexibility through hardware, software optimized for multiple input methods, and security that supports on-the-go workflows.

>[!NOTE]
> Copilot+ PC features are available only on select configurations with 16 GB of RAM or more; additional system requirements may apply. See more at [Windows 11 Specs and System Requirements](https://aka.ms/copilotpluspcspro).

## Hardware

Imagine that you're a traveling regional manager. You need a device that provides adequate power to run your productivity apps, but it must also offer portability to be able to get your work done from anywhere. Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) offers hardware that fits these needs. Let's explore some of the device specifications that solve this need without added complexity.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/snapdragon-x2-processor.jpeg" alt-text="Photo of the Qualcomm Snapdragon X2 Plus processor used in Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition).":::

**Processor:** The Qualcomm Snapdragon® powers Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) X2 Plus, a processor that delivers performance across full desktop workflows. It features a third-generation Qualcomm Oryon CPU with six cores running at a max frequency of 4.0 GHz—enabling AI-enhanced experiences and productivity apps to run efficiently. For added portability, it also features intelligent power efficiency—allowing users to operate on battery power for longer without performance interruptions.

**On-device AI:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) features an integrated Qualcomm Hexagon™ NPU (Neural Processing Unit) that delivers 80 TOPS (trillions of operations per second)—enabling on-device AI experiences like Click to Do, Improved Windows Search, and Live Captions. Additionally, AI acceleration in the NPU helps to enhance workflows for tasks like photo and video editing, multimodal content generation, and AI agents.

**Display:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) features a 12-inch PixelSense™ LCD display, featuring a resolution of 2196 x 1464 (220 pixels per inch). The display features a 3:2 aspect ratio, which is slightly taller than a standard 16:9 widescreen display—offering business users more vertical real estate, excellent for reading emails in Outlook or editing documents in Microsoft Word. The display on Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is anti-reflective and is ISO 9241-307 certified—enhancing visibility, reducing glare, and improving comfort for long working sessions, perfect for getting work done at the office or out in the field. Let's explore more about Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition)'s display:

| Size | 12-inch PixelSense™ LCD |
| :---: | :---: |
| Resolution | 2196 x 1464 (220 PPI) |
| Aspect ratio | 3:2 |
| Contrast ratio | 1400:1 |
| Refresh rate | Up to 90 Hz |
| Colors | Individually color-calibrated display with adaptive color and adaptive contrast |

| Brightness | 500 nits maximum |
| Coating | Anti-reflective, ISO 9241-307 certified |

>[!NOTE]
> Anti-reflective displays are exclusive to commercial versions of Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition).

**Ports and charging:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) features two USB-C®/USB 3.2 ports on the right side of the device. These ports offer support for charging, data transfer, Surface Thunderbolt™ 4 Dock and other accessories. For display expansion, Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) supports DisplayPort 1.4a with support for up to two 4-K monitors at 60 Hz refresh rate. Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) also features a magnetic housing for Surface Slim Pen at the back of the device and a connector at the bottom of the device for Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/surface-5g-connectivity.jpeg" alt-text="Photo illustrating 5G connectivity on Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition), utilizing touch screen, and inking capabilities.":::

**Connectivity:** To keep up with modern hybrid business needs, Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is equipped with updated connectivity technology. Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) features Wi-Fi 7, offering stable internet speeds, lower latency, and higher network capacity—ensuring that business users can access their most important documents wherever their work takes them. Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) also features Bluetooth® Core 5.4 technology enabling fast and reliable connections to essential accessories like keyboards and mice. On commercial models, Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) can also be configured with 5G connectivity—allowing business users to access cloud resources and real-time communication via their cellular provider when Wi-Fi is unavailable.

>[!TIP]
> Interested in learning more about the benefits of 5G connectivity on Surface for Business devices? Check out this [Learn module](/training/modules/surface-5g/).

**Input methods:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) allows business users to interact naturally with Windows and Copilot via multiple input methods. Users can interact with the 10-point multi-touch screen, trackpad, voice recognition, or with precise pen input using Surface Slim Pen. These various input methods enable business users to have device flexibility that matches their workflow. (Accessories sold separately)

**Key takeaway:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) combines AI-enabled performance, modern connectivity, and a portable design, allowing employees to remain productive whether they're working in the office, at home, or on the move. Features such as the Snapdragon processor, NPU, and 5G connectivity help ensure users can access the tools and information they need from virtually anywhere.

## Productivity experiences

In our scenario, you're a traveling regional manager looking to get tasks done in different work settings. The combination of hardware portability and optimized software on Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) helps you adapt to challenging environments. Let's briefly explore some of those features:

**Identify action items with Ink Canvas:** Ink Canvas is a lightweight workspace that lets users quickly capture notes, sketches, and ideas with Surface Slim Pen without switching to another application. Imagine that you're in a meeting and need to take notes for action items later. Pressing the Slim Pen tail button opens Ink Canvas, allowing you to jot notes naturally, circle key ideas, and ask Copilot to turn your annotations into action items and draft a follow-up email. Ink Canvas helps business users stay present in the meeting while simultaneously preparing next steps once the meeting concludes.

**Accomplish work in tablet mode:** You're reviewing a project status email in Outlook. Using Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition)'s improved touch capabilities, touchscreen-centric Windows productivity is more efficient. These improvements in Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) hardware enable navigation, scrolling, and task switching that's more fluid—enabling users to move quickly through content. Then, using Copilot to summarize key points, draft a response, or pull content directly into another file enables you to act without switching modes or waiting until you're connected to a keyboard.

**Secure access:** Consider a situation where you have a shared PC at home, but you take Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) with you to keep working throughout the day. With Windows Hello for Business, security is a primary focus, allowing you to open the device instantly without typing passwords or worrying who's nearby—even in public spaces. Your files are always up to date though OneDrive, and your sign-in and data are protected by Microsoft-built security from the moment you open your device. Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) allows you to move between home and everywhere else knowing your work is accessible, coordinated, and secure.

**Key takeaway:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) enables employees to work naturally with pen, touch, voice, and AI-powered experiences that reduce friction during everyday tasks. This flexibility helps users stay focused on their work instead of adapting to their device.

## Security

Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) includes security features that help protect business users while simplifying management for IT teams. Let's look at how the device delivers value for both groups.

- **Secured-core PC protection:** Windows 11 Secured-core PCs are designed to provide protection across hardware, firmware, and the operating system. For business users, this helps reduce exposure to modern threats while giving IT teams a secure foundation that is enabled out of the box.
- **Microsoft Pluton:** The Microsoft Pluton security processor integrates protection directly into the device silicon and works with enterprise-grade TPM 2.0 and BitLocker support. This helps protect sensitive business data, secure device identity, and support encryption policies without requiring extra hardware.
- **Windows Hello for Business:**  Windows Hello facial recognition with Enhanced Sign-in Security makes it easier for users to sign in quickly without entering a password. This improves the user experience while helping organizations reduce password-related risks, especially for employees working in shared or public environments.
- **NFC authentication:** Enables badge-based sign-in workflows that align with enterprise identity and access practices. For organizations that rely on physical credentials, this can help streamline secure access while supporting familiar workplace authentication habits.
- **Tested durability:** MIL-STD 810H testing for temperature, dust, humidity, and vibration help demonstrate that Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is designed for real-world work environments. For mobile employees, this added durability can help support productivity across offices, customer sites, travel, and other changing work conditions.
- **Extended device life:** Replaceable components available through authorized commercial resellers can help organizations maintain devices longer and simplify repair planning. This supports IT serviceability goals by helping reduce downtime and preserve device investments over time.

**Key Takeaway:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) combines Windows security features and Microsoft hardware protections to help safeguard business data without adding complexity for users. Organizations benefit from stronger protection, simplified management, and support for secure work across various environments.
