## Think in flow with Ink Canvas

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/ink-canvas.jpeg" alt-text="Photo showing Ink Canvas being used on Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) with Surface Slim Pen.":::

On Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition), business users have a wide range of input methods that they can utilize to accomplish their tasks. The 12-inch multi-touch touch screen, precision touchpad on Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard for Business and writing with Surface Slim Pen all present diverse ways to interact with the device. The combination of touch and pen input can best be utilized with Ink Canvas, a digital inking tool designed to help you stay in your workflow without switching applications.

Imagine you are in a Teams meeting. You need to take notes, develop action items, and send a follow-up email. With Canvas, Surface Slim Pen, and Copilot that multi-step process is streamlined. First, let's explore how to pair Surface Slim Pen to your Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition):

1. On Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition), select **Start** > **Bluetooth & devices** > **Add device** > **Bluetooth**.
2. Press and hold the top button of your pen for 5-7 seconds until the LED light flashes white to turn on **Bluetooth pairing mode**.
3. Select **Surface Slim Pen 2** to pair your pen.

Now that your Slim Pen is paired to Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition), you're ready to use Ink Canvas. During your meeting, press the tail button on your Slim Pen to open Ink Canvas. You now have space to jot down handwritten notes and circle points of emphasis with your Slim Pen. Because Ink Canvas has Copilot built in you can speak to Copilot to help organize your thoughts. Try the following prompt:

*"Turn this into action items and draft a follow-up email."*

Copilot reads your screen on Ink Canvas, understands the context, and organizes your notes, creates next steps, and helps you act—all without switching apps or attaching your keyboard. This workflow helps you stay present in the meeting and plan for your post-meeting obligations without losing your train of thought.

**Key takeaway:** Ink Canvas helps users capture ideas, organize information, and create next steps without interrupting their workflow. By combining digital ink with Copilot assistance, users can move from note-taking to action more efficiently.

## Completing work in tablet mode

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/tablet-mode.jpeg" alt-text="Photo showing Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) being used in tablet mode.":::

Windows 11 is moving towards more touch-based productivity. Recent Windows updates have added features such as direct handwriting in text fields, improved touch gestures, flexible taskbar positioning, and advanced haptic experiences. These improvements are especially evident on touch-first devices like tablets.

As a tablet device, Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is optimized for touch experiences. The display uses LCD2 technology, which is ideal for modern mobile workflows. Tablet mode, once considered best for consuming content, is optimized for productivity tasks.

You're reviewing a project status email in Outlook. Using touch gestures on Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition), you're able to scroll and highlighting a section. Then you can ask Copilot to summarize the risks and next steps and then drag the summary into PowerPoint to be used for an executive summary.

All of this is done with touch gestures in tablet mode, not requiring attaching to other accessories—ensuring that business users are able to stay productive in every situation with just touch input.

It's important to note that tablet mode in Windows 11 on Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) is automatic. It's turned on when your Surface is disconnected from the Surface Pro 12-inch Keyboard for Business or other Bluetooth peripherals. When those accessories are reattached or reconnected wirelessly, traditional laptop mode is activated.

**Key takeaway:** Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) demonstrates how modern Windows tablet experiences have evolved from content consumption to productivity-focused work. Users can review, create, and act on information using touch-first workflows without relying on a keyboard.

## Secure access on the go

You have a shared PC at home that you occasionally do some work from. But when it's time for you to work on the go, you take Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition). The challenge you face is to be able to continue the work you started on your home PC while accessing your files securely. On Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition), the combination of data access with OneDrive and Windows Hello for Business with Enhanced Sign-in enables business users to continue their workflows without friction even when their work location changes.

This is done through security that happens in the background. With Windows Hello for Business, you can open Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) instantly, even in public spaces, without the need to type in passwords or worry about who's nearby. Let's explore how to set up Windows Hello on your Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition):

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/account-sign-in-options.png" alt-text="Screenshot showing the account sign-in options in Windows 11 on Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition).":::

1. Open the **Settings app** on your Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition).
2. Select **Accounts**, then select **Sign-in options**.
3. Under **Ways to sign in**, select **Facial recognition (Windows Hello)**.
4. Select **Set up**.
5. Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) will prompt you to look directly at the screen.
6. Continue looking at the screen as Windows Hello registers your facial scan. For best results, it's best to have your face 6-8 inches away from the display.
7. Select **Finish** to complete the process.

With a Microsoft 365 subscription that includes OneDrive and is configured by your organization,  files are kept up to date across devices, and your sign-in and data are protected by Microsoft-built security from the moment you open the device. Let's review how to ensure that your important folders are backed up to OneDrive:

1. Select the **OneDrive cloud icon** in your notification area.
2. Select the **OneDrive Help & Settings icon**, then select **Settings**.
3. Navigate to the **Sync and backup** tab.
4. Select **Manage backup**.
5. To start backing up a folder, toggle any folder that says **Not backed up**, then select **Save changes**.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/onedrive-backup.png" alt-text="Screenshot showing the OneDrive backup settings in Windows 11 on Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition).":::

By enabling OneDrive backup and Windows Hello security, Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (2nd Edition) users have the peace of mind that their important data will be accessible and secure wherever their work takes them.

>[!NOTE]
> When your files finish synchronizing to OneDrive, they're backed up and you can access them from anywhere in Documents, or Desktop. When you back up your Desktop folder, the items on your desktop roam with you to other desktops where you're running OneDrive. If you're signed in to the OneDrive sync app on your computer, you can use File Explorer to access your OneDrive. You can also use the OneDrive mobile app to access your folders on other devices.

**Key takeaway:** Windows Hello for Business and OneDrive help users securely access their files and work across multiple locations. This combination reduces friction while helping ensure important business data remains protected and available when needed.
