Inclusive design starts with reducing barriers so more people can work comfortably, confidently, and productively. In this module, you'll explore how Surface for Business devices support inclusive hardware experiences through features such as haptic touchpads, Adaptive touch mode, and larger, high-contrast keyboard options. You'll learn how these design choices help users with different mobility, vision, and input needs interact with their devices reliably during everyday workplace tasks.

Why this matters: inclusive hardware helps organizations support a wider range of employees by reducing everyday barriers before they interrupt work. When input, feedback, and visibility are easier to personalize, users can spend less time adapting to the device and more time focusing on the task.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you'll be able to:

- Describe how inclusive hardware features on Surface for Business devices can reduce barriers for users with different mobility, vision, and input needs.
- Explain how Adaptive touch mode helps users customize touchpad interactions for reliable control.
- Identify how haptic touchpads provide consistent tactile feedback to support confidence and clarity during everyday tasks.
- Recognize how the Bold keyset supports users who benefit from larger, high-contrast, and tactile keyboard indicators.
