A keyboard can become a workplace barrier when key legends are difficult to see because of low lighting, visual fatigue, low vision, or another accessibility need. In those moments, users may slow down, make more typing errors, or lose confidence when switching between tasks.

On supported Surface Pro 13-inch keyboard options, the optional Bold keyset helps address this barrier with larger, high-contrast key legends and clearer visual separation. The tool is simple: make the keyboard easier to identify at a glance. The workplace outcome is faster, more confident typing during document creation, chat, email, and presentation work.

In addition to visual enhancements, the Bold keyset is optimized for those that rely on touch. Alongside the standard F and J key bumps, raised indicators are also added to the F4 and F8 keys to create reliable landmarks. There's also a tactile bump on the down arrow key, helping users distinguish direction by feel—which is especially useful for screen reader workflows.

These additions to Surface hardware are designed to fade into the background so users can stay in flow. Surface hardware supports more intuitive, inclusive interactions, helping to keep business users productive and efficient.
