A mouse can shape how someone moves through real work, such as reviewing a presentation, switching between apps, or preparing to share content in a meeting. Surface Mouse (2nd Edition) is designed to complement the Surface experience by combining a familiar navigation experience with integrated haptic feedback and customizable access to frequently used tools and workflows.

The mouse is intended to fit naturally in the hand, while quiet clicks and a compact form factor support everyday use. Integrated haptics provides tactile feedback during supported interactions in Windows and applications such as PowerPoint. A customizable action button also provides access to Copilot experiences and other frequently used shortcuts.

Together, these capabilities provide new ways to navigate, interact with content, and access commonly used tools from a mouse designed to complement Surface devices.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you'll be able to:

- Describe how the Surface Mouse (2nd Edition) redesigned form supports everyday use.
- Explain how integrated haptic feedback reinforces supported interactions in Windows and applications.
- Identify how the customizable action button provides access to Copilot experiences and other shortcuts.
- Determine which Surface Mouse (2nd Edition) capabilities best fit a specific workflow.
