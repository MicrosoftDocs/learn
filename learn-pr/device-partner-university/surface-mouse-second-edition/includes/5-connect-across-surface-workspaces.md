A Surface workspace may include more than one working context: a primary desk, a meeting room, a shared workspace, or a demo station. Surface Mouse (2nd Edition) is designed to support those shifts with multi-host connectivity and a compact form that travels easily between locations.

## Connect to multiple PCs

With Surface Mouse (2nd Edition), users can switch between connected devices by clicking the Bluetooth button on the bottom of the mouse. Doing so will show three lights that indicate which device the mouse is currently connected to.

Use this capability when your routine includes distinct work environments rather than a single fixed desk. For example, you might pair the mouse with a primary Surface device and a shared conference-room PC so your navigation experience stays consistent as the workspace changes.

When planning your setup:

1. **Identify the PCs you regularly use.** Determine whether your workflow spans one computer or multiple computers.
2. **Pair the mouse with the PCs you want to use.** The mouse supports connections with up to three computers and supports Swift Pair.
3. **Use multi-host support intentionally.** Choose this setup when one mouse would reduce extra peripherals or setup changes across those PCs.

## Consider the needs of your workspace

Different work environments can place different demands on a mouse. When deciding how Surface Mouse (2nd Edition) fits into your setup, consider the way you typically work. For example:

- If your day includes a primary desk and a shared conference-room PC, multi-host support can help you avoid switching to a different mouse for each space.
- If you frequently move between workspaces, the compact form factor can make the mouse easier to carry while retaining familiar mouse functionality.
- If you often work in shared or quiet environments, quiet switches can reduce clicking noise during meetings, calls, or focused work.
- If you want access to frequently used tools, the customizable action button can help reduce steps when opening Copilot experiences, Click to Do, Copilot Voice, or another supported shortcut.

## Put it into practice

Consider an IT admin who prepares a new hire device at a desk, then moves to a shared workspace to validate the setup with the employee. With the mouse paired to both PCs, the admin can move through setup and validation steps with the same familiar controls.

Before setting up the mouse, identify where the same pointing and scrolling experience would be most useful. This keeps the decision focused on the workflow rather than pairing other PCs by default.

**Key takeaway:** Surface Mouse (2nd Edition) is designed to complement different Surface workspaces. Multi-host support is most useful when it removes friction from a real routine, such as moving between a desk setup and a shared PC, rather than when it's treated as a feature to enable by default. The compact design and familiar controls help the mouse remain useful across those environments.
