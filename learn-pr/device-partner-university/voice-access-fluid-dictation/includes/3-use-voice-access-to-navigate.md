## Navigate quickly with Voice Access

Before you can update a document or respond to a stakeholder, you may need to move between applications, open files, review notes, or switch between windows. These tasks are small individually, but they take up limited time and focus.

Voice Access helps you control your Windows 11 PC using spoken commands, allowing you to initiate detailed actions in a natural way.

For example, you might use Voice Access to:

- Say "Open Microsoft Word" and then "Select Q4 report" to open the information you need for an upcoming meeting.
- Move between project documents and communication tools.
- Navigate directly between applications by asking for them by name, rather than manually searching through open windows or the taskbar.
- Access information without interrupting your workflow.

In short, Voice Access lets you use a natural tool—your voice—to reduce interface complexity, bringing you closer to the resources you need to get work done.

## Turn information into content with Fluid Dictation

Once you gather the information you need, the next step is often creating something to synthesize and share that insight. You could return to a traditional keyboard-based input at this stage or continue to explore voice interaction by using Fluid Dictation on your supported Copilot+ PC.

You might need to:

- Document a meeting outcome.
- Draft a stakeholder update.
- Capture customer feedback.
- Outline talking points for an upcoming presentation.

Fluid Dictation helps you create and edit those types of content and more with natural speech. Just select where you want to write using Voice Access or another input method and begin speaking naturally.

>[!NOTE]
> Fluid Dictation works by running small language models (SLMs) directly on your Copilot+ PC. These models enable your device to automatically correct grammar, add punctuation, and remove filler words as you speak to it in English, reducing the need for editing after the fact.

Rather than immediately focusing on typing, formatting, and editing, you can focus first on capturing ideas while they're still fresh. By speaking naturally, you can document decisions, action items, and key insights before attention shifts to the next task. Once the information is captured, you can review and refine the content as needed.

## A more connected workflow

Consider what happens after that roadmap review meeting.

First, you need to locate the relevant documents and customer feedback. Then, you need to summarize what was discussed and communicate next steps to stakeholders.

These activities are connected, but traditional workflows often treat them as separate tasks.

With Voice Access and Fluid Dictation, you can move more naturally from information gathering to content creation.

- You use Voice Access to find what you need.
- You use Fluid Dictation to act on what you learn.

Together, these capabilities support a workflow that feels more continuous and less fragmented.
