Imagine you're a product manager at a software company called Contoso. Your calendar is full of meetings, from roadmap reviews and stakeholder check-ins to customer feedback discussions and planning sessions. Between meetings, you need to capture decisions, update project plans, draft communications, and find information for your next discussion. You need to manage these tasks efficiently without losing focus.

Windows 11 Voice Access helps you control and navigate your PC using natural, spoken commands. On supported Copilot+ PCs, Fluid Dictation adds AI-assisted voice typing that can improve grammar, punctuation, and spelling as you speak. The capabilities can be used separately or together.

These capabilities can help you reduce context switching, stay focused, and complete common workplace tasks.

In this module, you learn how to use Voice Access and Fluid Dictation to navigate your PC, create content, and complete real-world productivity workflows.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you can:

- Navigate Windows and manage applications by using Voice Access on a Windows 11 PC.
- Create, capture, and refine content efficiently by using Fluid Dictation on a supported Copilot+ PC.
- Use Voice Access and Fluid Dictation together to complete common productivity tasks between meetings.
- Identify workplace scenarios where voice-powered workflows can help reduce context switching and improve productivity.
