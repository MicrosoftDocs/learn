As a product manager, your morning starts with a roadmap review. During the meeting, your team discusses customer feedback, adjusts priorities, and assigns several follow-up actions. As soon as the meeting ends, you realize your next stakeholder check-in begins in 10 minutes.

During those 10 minutes, you need to review notes, update a project document, answer a question from leadership, and prepare for the next conversation.

You have all the tools and knowledge to complete each task. The challenge is moving quickly enough between information, conversations, and content creation to keep work progressing throughout the day without losing too much time to frequent task-switching.

Voice-powered experiences can help reduce that friction.

## Turn small pockets of time into productive work

Think about how many times you move between applications during a typical day.

You might review customer feedback in one app, open meeting notes in another, check a project plan, and then draft a quick update for stakeholders. Copilot+ PCs support a more natural workflow by helping you navigate information and create content using your voice.

Rather than stopping to switch tools, find the right window, or type out every update manually, you can spend more time focused on the work itself.

## Two capabilities, one workflow

Voice Access and Fluid Dictation address different parts of the same productivity challenge:

- [Voice Access](https://support.microsoft.com/accessibility/windows/voice-access/get-started-with-voice-access), available on Windows 11 PCs, helps you move through your workday by enabling you to navigate Windows, switch between applications, and access the information you need through speech.
- [Fluid Dictation](https://support.microsoft.com/accessibility/windows/voice-access/fluid-dictation), available on supported Copilot+ PCs, improves voice-based authoring by suggesting corrections as you speak. It can adjust grammar, punctuation, and spelling in real time to help produce more readable dictated text.

>[!NOTE]
> Fluid Dictation requires a supported Copilot+ PC. Feature availability varies by device, language, region, release status, and installed Windows updates. Some functionality is available only in English.
> Always review content created with Fluid Dictation before sharing.

Individually, each capability can help streamline common workplace tasks. Together, they support a workflow that helps you move from gathering information to creating content without constantly changing how you interact with your PC.

Now imagine what happens after the Contoso roadmap review.

You locate customer feedback, review your notes, update a project document, and prepare talking points for your next meeting. Throughout that process, you're constantly moving between information and action, and that pattern repeats throughout the workday.

As you consider adding Voice Access and Fluid Dictation to your workflow, your goal isn't to replace every keyboard or mouse interaction. It's to make the tasks you perform dozens of times a day easier and more streamlined. Even small improvements may increase productivity over time.

## Key takeaways

- Modern productivity often requires moving quickly between information, conversations, and content creation.
- Small pockets of time between meetings can be valuable opportunities to complete meaningful work.
- Voice Access helps you navigate Windows and access the information you need on Windows 11 PCs.
- Fluid Dictation helps you capture ideas and create content using more natural speech on a supported Copilot+ PC.
- Together, these capabilities support a more seamless productivity workflow.
