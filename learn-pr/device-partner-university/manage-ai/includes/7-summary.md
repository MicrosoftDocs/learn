Organizations are increasingly adopting AI to help employees accomplish more while managing growing workloads. However, successful AI adoption requires more than deploying new capabilities. Organizations also need governance practices that help ensure AI experiences are secure, manageable, and aligned with organizational requirements.

In this module, you explored why governance is important when scaling AI adoption. You learned how governance helps organizations establish consistency, visibility, and oversight, and how Windows 11 works with solutions such as Microsoft Intune and Microsoft Agent 365 to support the deployment, management, and governance of AI experiences and agents.
Together, these capabilities help organizations establish a foundation for responsible AI adoption while maintaining control over how AI is introduced and used across the business.

## Key takeaways

Now that you've completed this module, you should be able to answer **"yes"** to the following statements:

- I understand why governance is important when scaling AI experiences.
- I can explain the benefits of establishing governance before broadly deploying AI tools.
- I can describe how Microsoft Intune helps manage AI experiences across Windows devices.
- I can explain how Microsoft Agent 365 helps organizations govern and manage AI agents.
- I can describe how governance supports responsible AI adoption at scale.
