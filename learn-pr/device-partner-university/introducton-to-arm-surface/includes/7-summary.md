In this module, you learned how Arm architecture supports efficient performance, long battery life, hardware-based security, and AI processing on Microsoft Surface devices. You also explored how Windows on Arm has evolved through native application support and Prism emulation.

Finally, you learned how to evaluate application and deployment readiness by reviewing application compatibility, confirming driver and management requirements, and using App Assure when compatibility assistance is needed. These considerations can help an organization determine whether ARM-based Surface devices are appropriate for its users and workflows.
