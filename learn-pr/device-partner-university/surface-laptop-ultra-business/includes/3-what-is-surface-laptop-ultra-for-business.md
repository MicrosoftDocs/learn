Microsoft designed Surface Laptop Ultra for Business for high performance, creative work, and intensive AI workloads. Copilot+ PCs combine AI-enabled hardware with experiences designed to improve productivity, creativity, and efficiency. Surface Laptop Ultra for Business supports existing workflows and new creative work that uses AI models in the cloud and on the device.

Surface Laptop Ultra for Business is designed for technical creators and power users who need high performance to build and create for their clients and organizations. The following specifications show how the device supports this work.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/surface-ultra-internals.png" alt-text="Photo of the internal components of Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra for Business.":::

**Processor and performance:** Surface Laptop Ultra for Business is powered by the NVIDIA RTX Spark™ N1X silicon platform. This processor features NVIDIA Blackwell RTX graphics, fusing AI and graphics performance in a single chip that delivers creation and AI development capabilities. It allows Surface Laptop Ultra for Business to retain a form factor that is designed for mobile work. Because this silicon platform doesn't require a discrete GPU (Graphics Processing Unit) module, thermals and weight are reduced while preserving performance capabilities.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/surface-ultra-cooling.jpeg" alt-text="Photo of the cooling system of Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra for Business.":::

Surface Laptop Ultra for Business supports configurations with up to 128 GB of Unified Memory. Professionals can choose a configuration based on their workloads. Higher memory configurations support local AI inference, 8K timeline scrubbing, and workflows that use multiple apps. The device also supports up to 2 TB of Gen 5 SSD storage to accelerate project load times and support asset-heavy work.

>[!NOTE]
> Unified Memory is memory shared between CPU and GPU.

**Display:** Surface Laptop Ultra for Business is equipped with a 15-inch PixelSense™ Ultra display with a resolution of 3270 x 2180 (262 pixels per inch) with a dynamic refresh rate of up to 120 Hz. Colors on the display are adaptive and accurate, tuned to the needs of creative workflows. Surface Laptop Ultra for Business scores a color accuracy of 100% on the DCI-P3 (Digital Cinema Initiatives — Protocol 3) color gamut and a D65 white point rating. This level of color accuracy is crucial for users in creative spaces working on digital assets.

The display also supports 10-point multitouch and has strengthened glass and an ISO 9241-307-certified anti-reflective coating. It reaches up to 2,000 nits of peak HDR brightness for previewing Dolby Vision and other HDR content. This brightness helps keep content visible in bright environments, such as studios and outdoor photo shoots.

>[!NOTE]
> Peak HDR is measured on a 10% window with HDR content. Sustained and SDR brightness are lower. Typical SDR brightness is 1,000 nits.

**Local AI:** Surface Laptop Ultra for Business uses its NVIDIA GPU to support demanding local AI workloads. Creators, developers, and data scientists can run large AI models, generate and upscale images, process video with AI effects, and refine AI-assisted workflows on the device.

With its mobile GPU optimized for AI, Surface Laptop Ultra for Business uses AI hardware and software technologies that support local execution of AI workloads that previously required more powerful computing resources. Surface Laptop Ultra for Business supports the GPU compute stack that AI developers and researchers already use, ensuring compatibility with popular machine learning frameworks and broader AI tools. All of this processing happens on-device, meaning that for supported local inference scenarios, AI processing can occur on the device rather than requiring information to be sent to a cloud-based AI service. This local inference eliminates cloud latency for more interactive workflow experiences. For organizations, this can help reduce reliance on cloud resources for suitable workloads while improving responsiveness and helping keep sensitive data under local control.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/surface-ultra-port-selection.jpeg" alt-text="Photo showing the port selection on Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra for Business.":::

**Ports and charging:** Surface Laptop Ultra for Business offers a wide selection of ports to minimize adapter use for creators. Surface Laptop Ultra for Business features three USB-C®/USB4® ports. These ports support up to 140W fast charging, 40 Gbps data transfer, and DisplayPort 2.1 with support for up to three 4K monitors at 60 Hz.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/multi-monitor-setup.png" alt-text="Photo showing a multi-monitor setup with Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra for Business with three monitors connected via the USB-C port.":::

Surface Laptop Ultra for Business also features an HDMI 2.1b port for further display expansion, USB-A 3.1 for peripherals, an SD card reader, and a 3.5mm headphone port. This suite of ports supports most common professional connections—including monitors, cameras, storage, and peripherals. By offering a wider range of port selection, Surface Laptop Ultra for Business allows business users to connect to their essential accessories without the need for adapters—enabling productivity and reducing complexity.

**Surface haptic touchpad:** Surface Laptop Ultra for Business features the largest Surface haptic touchpad to date. It is 30% larger than previous versions and supports precise input for creative work, gesture navigation, timeline scrubbing, and cursor control with haptic feedback. Adaptive touch lets users with varying mobility and dexterity needs customize how the touchpad responds.

Let's explore how to turn on this feature:

1. Open the **Surface app**.
2. Select **Touchpad settings**.
3. Enable Adaptive touch by toggling the switch to **On**.

**Security:** Surface Laptop Ultra for Business is designed for commercial environments that require enterprise-grade protection across hardware, firmware, and the operating system. As a Windows 11 Secured-core PC, it supports TPM 2.0 and BitLocker to help protect sensitive data and device identity, while Windows Hello facial recognition with Enhanced Sign-in Security provides a more secure sign-in experience for users. Together, these security features help organizations protect sensitive business data, reduce security risks, and support compliance requirements without adding complexity for end users.

Surface Laptop Ultra for Business also includes a memory-safe Secured Embedded Controller built with open-source ODP (Open Device Partnership) code. Its firmware foundation includes Surface UEFI built with Patina and Project Mu open-source components available through ODP, along with firmware built using Secured Embedded Controller project open-source components available through ODP. Firmware updates are delivered through Windows Update, helping IT teams keep devices current through familiar management processes.

**Serviceability:** Surface Laptop Ultra for Business is designed with commercial serviceability in mind, helping organizations extend device life, reduce downtime, and support repair workflows through replaceable components. Serviceable parts include:

- Display assembly (including camera)
- Keyboard assembly
- Touchpad
- Removable solid-state drive
- Battery
- Motherboard (including main processor and memory)
- Fan
- Speakers
- Enclosure
- Headphone port
- USB Mag-C port
- USB-C ports
- USB-A port
- Feet
- Screw kit
- Device Entry Kit

For IT teams, this modular approach can simplify maintenance planning and help keep devices in service longer by allowing qualified technicians to replace key components when needed.

**Key takeaway:** Surface Laptop Ultra for Business brings together high-performance hardware, advanced local AI processing, creator-focused display and connectivity options, accessible input features, and enterprise-grade security to support demanding professional workflows. These capabilities help technical creators and power users work with larger files, run more AI-assisted tasks on-device, and stay productive in secure commercial environments.
