Surface Laptop Ultra for Business is designed for professionals whose creative or technical work requires high performance and local AI processing. It is a fit for workloads that exceed everyday productivity needs and benefit from running directly on the device.

## Technical creators and AI developers

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/developer-use-case.jpeg" alt-text="Graphic representing a developer use case.":::

Surface Laptop Ultra for Business supports users who build, test, or refine AI-assisted experiences and need local computing resources. They might work with large language models, prototype AI features, or compare on-device inference with cloud-based inference.

**Example scenario:** A developer is building an internal support tool that summarizes long technical notes. They use Surface Laptop Ultra for Business with Foundry Local to test prompts, evaluate model responses, and refine the app experience without sending early project data to a cloud-hosted model.

The device also supports creators who work with high-resolution images, video, 3D assets, or design files. Its graphics capabilities, memory options, fast storage, and color-accurate display help with large, complex projects.

## Data scientists and research teams

The device can support users who analyze large datasets, test machine learning workflows, or evaluate AI models before scaling a solution to the cloud. Running suitable workloads locally can help teams iterate faster and reduce their reliance on cloud resources during early testing.

They use Surface Laptop Ultra for Business to run local tests, compare outputs, and validate a workflow before deciding which parts should move to a cloud environment for larger-scale processing.

## Enterprise users working with sensitive content

The device can support business users who want to process suitable source material locally as part of AI-assisted workflows. This can help teams work with confidential drafts, proprietary assets, or internal documents in a controlled environment.

**Key takeaway:** Consider Surface Laptop Ultra for Business when work requires more than everyday productivity performance. Its local AI processing, graphics capabilities, and business-ready security support demanding workflows for technical creators, AI developers, data professionals, and enterprise users.
