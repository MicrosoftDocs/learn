Surface Laptop Ultra for Business supports on-device AI workflows. Learn about its hardware, ports, and built-in capabilities before you turn it on. This information can help you connect accessories and use the device's performance and security features.

## Ports and powering on

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/surface-laptop-ultra-diagram.jpeg" alt-text="Diagram showing the ports and layout of Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra for Business.":::

Let's get familiar with the port selection on Surface Laptop Ultra for Business:

1. **Three USB-C®/USB4® ports:** Use these ports to charge the device or connect peripherals, such as monitors, portable drives, and docking stations. Keep the device plugged in during initial setup.

    >[!NOTE]
    > Surface Laptop Ultra for Business supports fast charging with a USB-C® charger rated for at least 140 W.

2. **USB-A 3.1 port:** Connect lower-power peripherals, such as mice, keyboards, storage devices, and webcams.
3. **SD card reader:** Read memory cards without an adapter or using another port.
4. **HDMI 2.1b:** Connect an external monitor that supports HDMI.
5. **3.5mm headphone port:** Connect wired headphones or speakers.

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/surface-laptop-ultra-front.png" alt-text="Photo of the front of Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra for Business showing the camera and display.":::

The front-facing Full HD Surface Studio Camera has 1080p resolution and supports Windows Studio Effects, including automatic framing, portrait blur, creative filters, eye contact, and background blur. Use it to join video meetings in apps such as Microsoft Teams.

Next to the Surface Studio Camera is the Windows Hello facial authentication camera with Enhanced Sign-in Security. After you set up Windows Hello, press the power button to sign in with facial recognition.

When you turn on your Surface, select your region and language, connect to a network, and sign in with your organization account. Signing in can sync files and data across devices and sign you in to apps such as Microsoft Edge.

After you sign in, you can set up Windows Hello, restore settings from a Microsoft account backup, and choose your privacy settings. Your Surface Laptop Ultra for Business is then ready to use.

>[!NOTE]
> If you don't set up Windows Hello during the initial device setup process, you can configure it at any time after signing into Windows by following these steps:
>
> 1. Open **Settings**.
> 2. Select **Accounts**.
> 3. Select **Sign-in options**.
> 4. Under **Ways to sign in** select **Facial recognition (Windows Hello)**.
> 5. Select **Set up**.
> 6. Follow the instructions to complete the configuration of Windows Hello.
>

**Key takeaway:** Surface Laptop Ultra for Business is designed to get users productive quickly with modern connectivity, simplified setup, and integrated collaboration features. Users can connect essential accessories, securely sign in, and start working with minimal configuration. These capabilities help organizations streamline deployment, reduce setup friction, and support a more productive user experience from day one.
