In this module, you explore how Copilot prompts can help you plan your day, prepare for employee conversations, collaborate more effectively with managers and teams, and stay organized from start to finish. These workflows can help you respond to employee requests more quickly, support manager follow-up, and keep HR work moving throughout the day.

- Plan and prioritize HR activities by using Microsoft 365 Copilot to organize meetings, employee requests, and administrative tasks.

- Use Copilot during meetings to capture key details, track action items, and maintain alignment with employees and managers.
