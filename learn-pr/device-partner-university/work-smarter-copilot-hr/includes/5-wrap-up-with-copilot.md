A productive HR day doesn't end when the last meeting is over. Reviewing employee requests, organizing follow-up activities, and preparing for tomorrow can help ensure employees and managers continue to receive timely support.

When prompted, Copilot can help you summarize your workday, organize priorities, and prepare for upcoming meetings so you can begin the next day with confidence.

## Review employee requests and priorities

At the end of the day, it's helpful to review employee conversations, outstanding requests, and any follow-up activities that still require attention.

Copilot in Outlook or Teams can help summarize your day and highlight the most important updates.

### Example prompt to review employee requests and priorities

*"Draft my end-of-day HR recap, including completed employee requests, outstanding actions, and my top three priorities for tomorrow."*

Copilot can help you:

- Review completed work and outstanding requests.
- Identify employee and manager follow-up activities.
- Keep important priorities visible for the next workday.

## Prepare for tomorrow

Organizing tomorrow's schedule helps ensure you have time for interviews, employee meetings, onboarding sessions, and administrative work.

Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat can help you review your calendar, identify available focus time, and draft a plan for reserving time for your highest-priority activities.

**Example prompt:**

*"Help me plan tomorrow morning by suggesting calendar blocks for onboarding preparation and employee meetings. Include reminders I should consider for upcoming interviews and outstanding HR tasks."*

Copilot can help you:

- Reserve time for important HR activities.
- Organize interviews, onboarding, and meetings.
- Keep upcoming priorities visible throughout the day.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Requires an eligible Microsoft 365 license. File upload and image generation limits apply.

## Get your device ready for tomorrow

As your workday comes to a close, you may want to adjust device settings to support your next work session.

Agent in Settings on your Copilot+ PC helps you describe what you want to change in natural language and find relevant device settings more quickly.

:::image type="content" source="../media/agent-settings-pc.png" alt-text="Screenshot of Agent in Settings on a Copilot+ PC using a natural language request.":::

### Example scenario to prepare your device for tomorrow

After finishing your workday, you want to restore your normal notification settings after completing focused work.

Using Agent in Settings, you can describe what you want to do in natural language, such as:

- *"Turn off Focus mode."*
- *"Restore standard notifications."*

Agent in Settings can help you quickly locate and adjust settings without navigating through multiple menus.
