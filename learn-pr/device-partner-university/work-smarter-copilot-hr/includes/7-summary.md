In this module, you explored how Copilot prompts and Copilot+ PC experiences can support human resources professionals throughout their day—from planning and employee support to meetings and end-of-day organization.

## Important key takeaways

- Microsoft 365 Copilot can help you plan and prioritize HR activities by organizing meetings, employee requests, and administrative tasks.
- Copilot can help you prepare employee summaries, create onboarding content, and draft clear communications that align with HR policies.
- Copilot in Teams can help you capture meeting details and draft follow-up actions for review.
- Copilot+ PC experiences such as Click to Do, Live Captions, Studio Effects, and Agent in Settings can support communication, content refinement, and device-level workflows.
- For employee-impacting scenarios, review Copilot-generated content for accuracy, relevance, permissions, and alignment with your organization's HR, privacy, accessibility, and legal review requirements.

By using Copilot and Copilot+ PC experiences together, you can spend less time on repetitive administrative work, stay organized across your workflow, and focus more on timely employee support, manager alignment, and clearer follow-up.
