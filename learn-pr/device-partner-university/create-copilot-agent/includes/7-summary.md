In the example scenario, a sales team regularly reviews customer information, product materials, and previous notes to prepare for meetings. Repeating this process can take time and make it difficult to prepare consistently.

## Key takeaways

In this module, you:

- Identified customer meeting preparation as a focused, repeatable task for a first agent.
- Created the Customer Meeting Prep agent with a defined purpose, reusable instructions, relevant knowledge, and an expected output.
- Tested the agent with typical, incomplete, and out-of-scope requests to evaluate its responses.
- Used the test results to refine the agent's instructions and improve the consistency of its meeting briefs.

Without an agent, sales team members might need to gather information, repeat instructions, and organize each meeting brief manually. The quality and structure of the results can also vary from one meeting to the next.

A focused agent provides a reusable and consistent starting point for customer meeting preparation. By combining a clear purpose, reusable instructions, and relevant knowledge sources, it can help reduce repeated setup and make important information easier to review. Users remain responsible for confirming that the agent's output is accurate, appropriate, and ready to use.
