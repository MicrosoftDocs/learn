AI agents introduce a new governance challenge for organizations. Unlike AI experiences that only generate responses, agents can access systems, use tools, interact with applications, and take actions on behalf of users. As organizations adopt more agents, they need a consistent way to govern those activities while maintaining visibility, control, and accountability.

In this module, you explored how an enterprise governance strategy can help organizations prepare for AI agent adoption. You learned how governance controls can be applied through identity, containment, and management, how organizations can evaluate risk based on an agent's capabilities and potential impact, and how the control plane and execution layer work together to govern agent activity.

Together, these concepts provide a foundation for scaling AI adoption while maintaining appropriate governance, security, and oversight.

As organizations continue expanding their use of AI agents, governance becomes an important part of adopting AI responsibly. By establishing visibility into existing agents, defining governance requirements early, and aligning endpoint investments with future needs, organizations can scale AI adoption with greater confidence while maintaining appropriate oversight and control.
