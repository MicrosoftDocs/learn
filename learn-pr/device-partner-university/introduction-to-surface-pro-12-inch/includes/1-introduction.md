Finding the right device for your business needs is often challenging, and the way you work needs to be considered. This module introduces the Microsoft Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (1st Edition)—a Copilot+ PC built by Microsoft that supports various usage scenarios, including small business and higher education applications.

## Learning objectives

- Evaluate whether Microsoft Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (1st Edition) is appropriate for your business.
- Describe how the software and hardware features of Microsoft Surface Pro for Business, 12-inch (1st Edition) work to complete more tasks.
