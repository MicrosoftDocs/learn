In today's business landscape, the incorporation of AI tools is evolving at a rapid pace. With that constant change, comes the need for rethinking the architecture of computer hardware to accommodate new tools like on-device AI and agents. This module introduces NVIDIA RTX Spark— for the era of personal AI agents.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you'll be able to:

- Describe how NVIDIA RTX Spark™ hardware supports local AI, creative, and technical workloads on Windows PCs.
- Explain how unified memory, GPU performance, and FP4 (four-bit floating point) AI compute contribute to responsive on-device AI experiences.
- Identify how personal AI agents can help users delegate multi-step workflows while maintaining control over data and outputs.
- Recognize the user personas and scenarios that benefit most from RTX Spark™-powered devices.
