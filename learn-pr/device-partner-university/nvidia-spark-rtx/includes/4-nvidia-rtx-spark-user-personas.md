NVIDIA RTX Spark™ is built for a new generation of Windows PCs that can handle various workloads, from everyday work to demanding AI, creative, technical, and gaming applications. With high-performance local AI compute and up to 128 GB of unified memory, RTX Spark™ gives users more headroom to create, build, play, and run sophisticated AI-driven workflows locally.

## Software Developers and AI Engineers

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/software-developer.png" alt-text="Photo of a software developer at a desk working on a computer.":::

Developers using AI coding assistants often run into latency and privacy issues when connecting to remote cloud servers. With RTX Spark™, developers can host and fine-tune large models locally on their primary machine.

**Primary Driver:** Unmetered compute and zero cloud API costs.

**Key Workflows:**

- **Local Codebases & Indexing:** Running autonomous background coding agents (like Cursor, Claude Code, or Hermes Agent) that index full repositories without sending confidential code outside the local network.
- **Model Prototyping:** Testing and fine-tuning models up to 120 billion parameters locally before deploying to production.
- **Real-time Autocomplete & Debugging:** Zero-latency code generation directly on-device, even when working offline or in air-gapped environments.

## Creative Professionals and Content Creators

Designers, video editors, and 3D artists frequently deal with severe hardware bottlenecks when working with high-resolution assets.

**Primary Driver:** Large memory unified across CPU/GPU with local AI rendering.

**Key Workflows:**

- **3D creation and rendering:** Create and render complex 3D scenes with RTX technologies including NVIDIA OptiX and DLSS, improving the performance and quality of ray traced interactive 3D workflows.
- **Video and content creation:** Edit and enhance content with RTX Video and NVIDIA Broadcast, with hardware-accelerated capabilties that sharpen video, smooth motion, and isolate your voice in real-time.
- **Local generative AI:** Run image and video generation models locally, allowing creators to extend shots, improve audio edits, or pre-visualize shoots, all while keeping their work private on their PCs.
- **On-the-Go Production:** Getting powerful performance in lightweight, thin-and-light laptop form factors with all-day battery life.

## Enterprise Knowledge Workers in High-Compliance Industries

:::image type="content" border="true" source="../media/enterprise-knowledge-workers.png" alt-text="Photo of an enterprise knowledge worker at a desk reviewing documents on a laptop.":::

Financial analysts, legal teams, healthcare researchers, and management consultants work daily with highly sensitive information that cannot be uploaded to public cloud AI services.

**Primary Driver:** Data sovereignty, IP (intellectual property) protection, and privacy-first local reasoning.

**Key Workflows:**

- **Deep Document Intelligence:** Running local agents that parse, summarize, and cross-reference hundreds of confidential PDFs, financial statements, or patient records simultaneously.
- **Private Workflow Automation:** Delegating cross-application administrative workflows (updating spreadsheets, drafting Outlook emails, generating deck outlines) under strict OS sandboxing.
- **Compliance Assurance:** Leveraging Windows Agent Primitives and NVIDIA OpenShell to ensure sensitive business logic stays strictly within the hardware perimeter.
